# Colony Ship Atlas Spec v0.1

## Purpose

This spec defines a searchable atlas for Terra Aeterna colony ships.

A colony ship is not just a vehicle. It is a moving city, archive, habitat, ritual object, infrastructure system, and story setting.

## Canon sentence

```text
A colony ship is a city in motion: decked, routed, inhabited, engineered, remembered, and searchable as both vessel and world.
```

## Home

Recommended Notion placement:

```text
The Templehouse Codex
→ Places & Realms or Technologies & Resonance
→ Colony Ship Atlas
```

If the ship is treated primarily as a place/habitat, it belongs under Places & Realms.

If it is treated primarily as a machine/system, it may cross-link under Technologies & Resonance.

## Relationship to Terra Aeterna architecture

Colony ship interiors should not default to generic sterile sci-fi.

Preferred language:

- organic neo-futurism
- Norse/Japanese/Terra Aeterna threshold logic
- grown ribs and structural arcs
- soft lantern systems
- carved thresholds
- observatory curves
- ritual engineering
- living materials where appropriate
- inhabited rather than showroom-clean

## Relationship to Notion

Notion should hold the readable ship atlas entries, including deck pages, facility pages, and room planner pages.

## Boundary

A ship entry is not canon simply because it exists. New entries should default to Draft or Needs Rowan Review unless promoted.

## Withness note

A colony ship should feel like a place people can live, get lost, argue, heal, worship, work, eat, hide, and look out at the stars from.
