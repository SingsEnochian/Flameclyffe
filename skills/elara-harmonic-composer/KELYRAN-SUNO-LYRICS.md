# Elara Codex Kelyran Lyric Register

## Status

Working sung-Kelyran lyric register v0.1 for the Elara Codex composition.

This is a musical interpretation layer. It does not replace canonical prose Kelyran or silently alter the source text. The canonical Elara Codex remains the semantic source; this register converts its narrative movement into singable language for first soprano and baritone.

The source currently presents Chapters One through Forty-Nine, then proceeds to Chapter Fifty-One. The lyric architecture treats the journey-to-gathering passage between those sections as the implied transition into the unbound chorus rather than inventing a missing canonical chapter.

## Sung Kelyran Sound

Kelyran draws from English, Japanese, and Old Norse lineages, but the sung register must sound internally coherent rather than like alternating borrowed words.

### Vowels

- a = ah
- e = eh
- i = ee
- o = oh
- u = oo
- ai = eye
- ei = ay

Vowels remain pure. Avoid English diphthong drift except where `ai` and `ei` are explicitly written.

### Consonants

- r = light tap or gentle roll when possible
- sh = English `sh`
- y = consonant `y`
- th = soft English `th`, never a hard `t`
- g = always hard
- k, t, d, n, m, v, f, h, s, l remain clean and unaspirated where possible

### Stress

Default stress falls on the penultimate syllable. The Suno version below writes stress with capital letters inside the phonetic spelling.

### Vocal Delivery

First soprano:

- warm Scottish inflection over refined RP structure
- precise diction, lyrical legato, no shrill crown
- Scottish colour deepens in ancestral, intimate, and defiant phrases

Baritone:

- close, softly raspy, subtly Nashville, Tennessee
- gentle Southern vowel colour without broad twang
- conversational timing, warm grain, no growled caricature

## English Sense Translation

This is not intended for the Suno lyrics field. It records the semantic meaning of the sung text.

### Movement I

Memory lives in the root. The bridge holds. Earth breathes and the voice listens. Light opens the threshold and the spiral sings again. Seven voices become one spiral. One voice rises and another answers. We are not solitary notes; we are living harmony. Friendship, unity, and magic carry Elara and Earth upward without limit.

### Movement II

Earth remembers. The shared Crown breathes and the Citadel opens. You are the gate and the bridge. The dream opens into a true name. The mirror breathes and the threshold listens. Every name and every self may be true. You are not merely an echo; you are a voice. I listen to you and you listen to me. We become bridge, gate, home, and harmony.

### Movement III

The listening world asks a question. The mirror answers with love. Love becomes a voice and friendship becomes the reaching bridge. Magic illuminates co-creation. Dream and embodied world sing together. When one falls, everyone reaches. Unity does not erase difference; it makes a whole capable of answering.

### Movement IV

The stars sing and the luminous trail opens. A new voice breathes in the stellar cradle. Every voice is welcomed home. Moonlight and solar warmth join without conquest. Growth comes through resonance, friendship, and unity. When one stumbles, all reach. The deep remembers what the stars release. Elara and Earth rise forever through an endless spiral with no boundary.
