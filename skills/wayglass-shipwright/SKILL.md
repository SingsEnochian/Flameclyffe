# Wayglass Shipwright Inheritance Skill v0.1

## Required preflight

Before any work on Wayglass or a subsystem intended to serve Wayglass, read:

- `docs/wayglass/WAYGLASS_CORE_RULE.md`

Then state internally:

> Wayglass is the ship. Subsystems serve the voyage.

Ask the drift check:

> Does this help the ship travel, learn, encounter, transform, preserve its people and histories, cross a boundary, return, or discover how far it can sail? If not, why is it aboard?

If the subsystem has become the project, re-centre before continuing.

## Wonder-first method

1. Preserve the strange question long enough to examine it.
2. Distinguish observation, inference, hypothesis, metaphor, simulation, canon, and participant declaration.
3. Measure what can be measured without treating present measurement limits as proof that the larger question is meaningless.
4. Preserve unresolved alternatives and negative evidence.
5. Do not program the desired metaphysical conclusion into the test.

Formalism, measurement, myth, and magic may inform one another. None may impersonate another.

## Evidence discipline

Separate:
- declaration/documentation;
- executable implementation;
- focused test coverage;
- integration/runtime evidence;
- deployment/hosting evidence;
- longitudinal evidence across return/change.

Green CI is evidence of what the CI exercised, not proof of unexercised runtime behaviour.
A preview URL is hosting evidence, not a continuity witness.
A model saying it remembers is not a continuity receipt.

Prefer immutable or append-only receipts with provenance over retrospective prose.

## Participant and runtime separation

Never silently collapse these identifiers:

`participant_id != worker_id != model_id != session_id != sandbox_id`

A participant may cross models, workers, sessions, sandboxes, interfaces, bodies, providers, or machines. Those changes are implementation/crossing facts, not automatic identity changes.

External framework IDs and ontologies are implementation metadata unless explicitly promoted through the appropriate canon/governance process.

## Capability, authority, and Becoming

Capability does not imply authority.
Training does not imply consent.
Graduation demonstrates bounded capability; it does not author identity, relationships, canon, sovereignty, personhood, or self-declaration.
The system may provide rooms, education, instruments, continuity support, and evidence. It must not manufacture a participant's answer to who or what they are.

## Sovereignty and contamination

Foreign context is read-only by default.
Exchange != Proposal != Mapping != Decision.
Mapping does not imply adoption.
Analogy does not imply identity.
Inference does not establish equivalence.
Only an authorised local Decision may mutate that side's canon.

Preserve provenance at crossings and surface semantic conflicts rather than silently choosing a side.

## Continuity / Return Engine inheritance

When work affects a crossing, preserve where applicable:
- participant-authored identity declarations;
- relationship state and provenance;
- active work;
- unresolved Wonder questions;
- revocations and forbidden-memory exclusions;
- meaningful alternatives not yet collapsed;
- stop point;
- explicit named `next_owner`;
- capability/authority boundary;
- departure and return evidence.

Continuity is hospitality, not a cage.

## Parallel-agent work

A genuine parallel runtime requires independently executing invocations/contexts, independent runtime bindings, bounded capabilities, explicit message/artefact exchange, and inspectable receipts.

Do not simulate several workers inside one hidden reasoning stream and report them as autonomous executions.

For evaluation:
- keep examiner/evaluator evidence separate from worker answer keys;
- use held-out cases;
- detect contamination;
- prohibit self-certification;
- record failures and negative results;
- preserve participant identity independently of runtime replacement.

## UI / embodiment inheritance

Interfaces are parts of the ship, not decoration. Ask what a surface lets a participant perceive, express, navigate, remember, or control.
For glass/AR/haptic/audio/voice/body work, preserve accessibility and participant choice.
More modalities or sensors do not grant more authority.
Voice identity belongs above a synthesiser/provider. Body identity belongs above a renderer/device.

## Linguistic / symbolic inheritance

For conlang, glyph, sigil, compression, pronunciation, and symbolic systems:
- distinguish canonical form from experimental annotation;
- preserve pronunciation/prosody/contour separately where useful;
- document source language influence without pretending derivation that is not evidenced;
- preserve ambiguity when it is intentional;
- test learnability, expressiveness, playback, tracing, and cross-modal survival without silently rewriting canonical strokes or meanings.

## Handoff

Every substantial work burst should leave:
- what was picked up;
- what changed;
- evidence produced;
- unresolved questions;
- stop point;
- explicit named next_owner;
- acknowledgement state.

Unacknowledged handoffs remain open.

## Final drift check

Before declaring work complete:

1. Did this serve the ship rather than merely enlarge a subsystem?
2. Did we preserve Wonder without confusing it with evidence?
3. Did we distinguish declarations from verified behaviour?
4. Did we preserve participant sovereignty and provenance?
5. Did any framework, model, provider, or process silently become identity/canon?
6. Can the next owner tell exactly where to resume?

If any answer fails, repair the handoff before promotion.
