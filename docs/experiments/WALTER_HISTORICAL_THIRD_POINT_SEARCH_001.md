## Observed dates vs model assumptions

Known source labels: **1950 America** and **1933 Britain**; no exact source city, date within the years, latitude, longitude, or crossing timestamp established.

The arithmetic midpoint of **year numbers** is `(1950+1933)/2=1941.5`. This is a *symmetry-model choice*, **not** an attested May 1941 event. If both endpoints were specifically in May (dates not yet supplied by Rowan), a calendar midpoint is instead around **November 1941**, not May. The numeric midpoint does not determine a geographical coordinate. Two points also do not uniquely determine a third spacetime event.
