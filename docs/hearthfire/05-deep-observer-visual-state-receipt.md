## Boundaries

- Readings show inputs; they do not prove hidden states.
- Sound is generated locally after activation.
- Exports remain local unless the user copies or saves them.
- Haptics depend on supported browser/device features.
- Browser fallbacks should preserve the instrument rather than breaking it.
