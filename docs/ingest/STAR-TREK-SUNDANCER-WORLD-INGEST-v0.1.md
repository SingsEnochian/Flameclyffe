# Vulcan naming ingest

## Canon tendencies

Classic Trek frequently uses masculine Vulcan names beginning with **S** and ending with **k**, often five letters: **Spock, Sarek, Surak, Sybok, Satok**.

Many feminine Vulcan names use **T’** forms, especially **T’P-** forms: **T’Pau, T’Pring, T’Pol**.

These are **patterns, not hard linguistic laws**. Canon contains substantial variation including **Tuvok, Vorik, Solok, Stonn, Koss, Mestral, Valeris, Saavik, Sakonna, T’Rina, T’Lyn, Xon**.

Do not sprinkle apostrophes randomly. T’ is a recurring naming pattern, not generic alien decoration.

# Trill naming ingest

## Canon structure

Joined Trill commonly use **HOST PERSONAL NAME + SYMBIONT NAME**.

Examples: **Jadzia Dax, Ezri Dax, Curzon Dax, Lenara Kahn, Gray Tal, Jinaal Bix**.

Hosts may have separate pre-joining family names, e.g. **Ezri Tigan** and **Joran Belar**.

Joining produces a synthesis of host and symbiont, not simple possession.

### Canon seed names

Dax lineage: **Lela, Tobin, Emony, Audrid, Torias, Joran, Curzon, Jadzia, Ezri**.

Other seed names: **Verad, Lenara, Nilani, Gray, Jinaal**.

Symbiont examples: **Dax, Kahn, Tal, Bix, Odan**, plus series symbiont **Xiiv**.
