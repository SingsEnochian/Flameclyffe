## Core vocabulary

### Lattice

Grinberg proposes a fundamental informational structure of space in which each point contains information from the whole. Objects, forces, fields, thoughts, and emotions are treated as local organisations or distortions of that structure.

### Sintergia

A neologism from *síntesis* and *energía*. It denotes coherence, informational density, convergence, and homogeneity. High Sintergia is described as coherent and highly convergent; low Sintergia as less coherent and more locally distorted.

### Neuroalgoritmización

The brain's concentration of information from multiple sources into increasingly inclusive codes. Grinberg describes this operation as digital and associates it with thought, conceptualisation, and language.

### Campo Neuronal

A proposed analog experiential field generated by the brain's activity and geometry. In the theory, experience emerges through interaction between the Campo Neuronal and the Lattice.

### Orbitales de la Conciencia

Bands of congruent interaction between the Campo Neuronal and the Lattice. Congruent bands yield cleaner interference patterns; incongruent bands yield noise or self-cancellation.

### Hipercampo

The Lattice as it includes all neuronal fields and their intercerebral interference patterns. The Hipercampo Expandido also includes nonhuman neuronal fields.

### Factor de Direccionalidad

The focal mechanism determining which portion or level of experience becomes conscious.

### Duración del presente

The temporal integration window of experience. Higher-order neuroalgorithmic processing incorporates events from different temporal orders into a broader present.

### Atractor Extraño del Futuro Ideal del Hipercampo

A proposed future attractor guiding increasing complexity, centrality, and unification.
