## Central question

How does neuronal activity become qualitative experience?

Grinberg's model proposes that the nervous system transforms information through neuroalgorithmisation, generates a neuronal field, and interacts with an informational structure of space called the Lattice. Experience is said to arise from the resulting interference pattern.

## Bilingual concept ledger

### Lattice
**Spanish use:** *Lattice, enrejado, celosía*  
**English working sense:** informational lattice or matrix of space  
**Theory role:** proposed substrate containing distributed information and capable of coherence, distortion, and reorganisation.

### Sintergia
**Formation:** *síntesis + energía*  
**English:** syntergy  
**Theory role:** degree of coherence, informational density, convergence, and homogeneity.

### Bandas Sintérgicas
**English:** syntergic bands  
Discrete strata or families of Lattice organisation associated with qualities or levels of consciousness.

### Neuroalgoritmización
**English:** neuroalgorithmisation  
Progressive concentration of information from many sources into increasingly inclusive neural codes.

### Neurosintergia
**English:** neurosyntergy  
Degree of coherence, informational density, convergence, and multidimensional integration in cerebral processing.

### Campo Neuronal
**English:** neuronal field  
Proposed analog field produced by brain activity and geometry. Its interaction with the Lattice is said to produce experience.

### Orbitales de la Conciencia
**English:** orbitals of consciousness  
Levels or bands in which Neuronal Field and Lattice interact with varying congruence.

### Hipercampo
**English:** hyperfield  
The Lattice as it includes all neuronal fields and their intercerebral interference patterns. The expanded Hyperfield includes nonhuman neuronal fields.

### Factor de Direccionalidad
**English:** factor of directionality  
Focusing mechanism determining what portion, level, or location of experience becomes conscious.

### Duración del presente
**English:** duration of the present  
Temporal integration window within which events from different moments are unified into one experienced present.

### Observador
**English:** observer  
Witnessing reference that can become less identified with changing mental, emotional, bodily, and perceptual contents.

### Neuroalgoritmización Expandida
**English:** expanded neuroalgorithmisation  
Increasingly inclusive observation integrating more contents and higher levels of abstraction.

### Atractor Extraño del Futuro Ideal del Hipercampo
**English:** strange attractor of the Hyperfield's ideal future  
Proposed future-oriented organising attractor in Grinberg's practical model of collective directionality.

## Argument chain

1. Perception is a transformed representation, not a simple copy.
2. The brain performs convergent information processing through neuroalgorithmisation.
3. Brain activity also generates a proposed analog neuronal field.
4. The neuronal field interacts with the proposed Lattice.
5. Their interference pattern gives rise to qualitative experience.
6. Degrees of congruence correspond to orbitals or levels of consciousness.
7. Attention selects among possible contents through the Factor of Directionality.
8. Individual fields interact within a larger Hyperfield.
9. Development involves greater inclusion, coherence, temporal integration, and unity.
