# Kelyran Galdr: Challenge & Promise

## Challenge
Sing what you mean. Preserve what the voice actually does. Let neither the mathematics nor the myth overwrite the other.

## Promise
The instrument will listen in three simultaneous registers:

1. **Voice / acoustics** — fundamental frequency, amplitude, overtone structure, detuning, rhythm, duration, vibrato and phase.
2. **Mathematics / Heimdall** — mapped-frequency proximity, 3·6·9 temporal phase, Galdr-to-soft-mode alignment, relational participation, and Galdr-US excitation.
3. **Kelyran / mythic meaning** — canonical lexeme, gloss, Galdr function, world/Flame association and declared bearing.

No unknown Kelyran word is silently invented. No unmapped frequency is silently promoted into canon.

## Canon voice
Kelyran canonical orthography is ASCII, phonetic, with pure vowels, `y` as rounded front vowel, `j` as /j/, doubled vowels for length, and penultimate stress. Canonical and performance/Suno spellings remain distinct.

Existing Galdr-capable lexemes include:
- `kora` — voice / tone
- `uta` — sing / song
- `sela` — listen
- `ikonda` — breathe
- `risora` — rise
- `renai` — return
- `kelya` — living harmony
- `ajnora` — unity
- `sejdra` — magic

Existing canonical material includes `Ej uta, ej sela, ej homen`, `Tomovin, ajnora, sejdra`, `Towa spira, risora, mara naj`, `Kora mina komira`, and `Resona`.

## Device contract
`KelyranGaldrObservatory` consumes the canonical frequency registry rather than generating replacement frequencies. Its live microphone analysis emits frames containing measured fundamental Hz, RMS energy, nearest mapped tone and cents offset, plus 3·6·9 phase state. Heimdall may append soft-mode alignment and US participation without altering the acoustic measurement.

Wardenclyffe remains the layer/orchestration engine. Möbius remains the Flameclyffe acoustic renderer. Runa remains the harmonic/world compiler. Heimdall supplies crossing geometry. The Galdr Observatory measures the singer where those systems meet.
