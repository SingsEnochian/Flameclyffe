## Why this matters

Young Yggdrasil should understand that the Luna project is not a generic rejected-mate werewolf romance. It is a restoration epic about sacred jurisdiction, consent, witness, healing, and the lawful refusal of corrupted power.

This seed should be read after the larger Terra Aeterna and Templehouse layers, and alongside the Luna series wiki.

## Core lock

**The wolf is not owned by blood. The wolf is entrusted by the Moon.**

Luna is not a decorative mate-title. Luna is an office, a jurisdiction, and a sacred legal authority. A Luna may be a mate, but matehood does not create Luna authority. The office is older than modern Alpha hierarchy.

## Luna Law

Luna Law is the oldest surviving sacred-legal tradition among wolfkind. It predates hereditary Alpha dynasties and teaches that the wolf is entrusted through bond, conduct, and lawful relation.

An Alpha's authority is real but conditional. He may lead hunts, defend territory, command warriors, settle disputes, and speak for the pack in danger. He may not compel the soul, corrupt the mate-bond, silence a healer, punish refusal, break oath-right, or claim divine sanction for domination.

Those matters belong to Luna jurisdiction.

## The Rite of Refusal

The highest and most feared act of Luna Law is the Rite of Refusal, by which a true Luna may deny an Alpha access to his wolf.

This does not destroy the wolf. It calls the wolf back behind the Moon's veil, severing the Alpha's access until judgement, repentance, healing, or final exile is complete.

Eira does **not** steal wolves. She withholds access when Alpha authority has broken Moon Law. This distinction is central. Her power is not domination in reverse. It is lawful refusal.

## Eira Catrine Windmere

Eira is dangerous because she is a survivor of a system that forgot what she is. Windmere treated Luna as pack property, then Eira reveals Luna as jurisdiction.

Her healer training matters because she does not begin by punishing. She begins by diagnosing. A Luna healer can tell whether a wound belongs only to flesh, or whether the body is carrying oath-damage, bond injury, or law-rot.

Her arc should move from surviving Windmere to restoring Luna jurisdiction. She is underestimated because Windmere mistakes size, polish, rank, and violence for authority.

## Windmere's corruption

Windmere's corruption is institutional amnesia, not only cruelty. It preserved fragments of Luna Law as ceremony, healer custom, marriage rite, mourning song, and court language, but bent the living authority into ornament.

Modern Windmere kept the vessels and forgot the teeth.

## Rhydian's core offence

Rhydian Maelor Caerwyn is not merely a cruel Alpha. His central offence is the belief that Alpha power justifies itself. He treats authority as self-validating, blood-backed, and socially enforceable.

Under Luna Law, this is precisely the lie that must be brought to judgement.

## Three-moon court

The sky is witness and court.

- **Aurel:** pale-gold ringed moon; high Luna authority, lawful witness, true bonds, transformation, and divine court.
- **Glaswren:** green agate ancestor moon; beloved dead, burial groves, grief-healing, memory, old names, and healer visions.
- **Mawr:** deep mauve broken moon; consequence, oath-rot, wolf-denial, exile, sentencing, and the fate of wolves who refuse restoration.

Old triad:

> Glaswren remembers.  
> Aurel witnesses.  
> Mawr answers.

## Moonmere Gate

Moonmere Gate is not a convenience portal. It is witness. It is the hinge where Windmere's private crimes become visible to a wider cosmos.

The Gate opens to Luna authority, not Alpha blood. When Eira calls down the moon, she reactivates an interworld treaty older than Windmere's current pack law. Terra Aeterna becomes sanctuary, archive, witness, and diplomatic pressure.

## Consequences of wolf-denial

Wolf-denial should land on four levels:

- **Sensory:** the body becomes too quiet; scent dulls; hearing flattens; the shift will not answer; the pack feels a wrong silence around him.
- **Legal:** Alpha authority enters judgement; commands may still be obeyed socially, but sacred validity is suspended.
- **Magical:** corrupted Alpha-runes may fail, blacken, crack, bleed lakewater, or lose their answering wind.
- **Social:** the pack panics, remembers, fractures, or feels relief. The world has heard the court bell.
