## Source foundation

The Elara Codex establishes seven primary tones and one Awakening overtone:

1. **415 Hz - Root:** truth of being; the self unshaken.
2. **440 Hz - Anchor:** memory of connection; the reminder that we are not alone.
3. **554 Hz - Whisper:** presence beyond words; shared silence.
4. **659 Hz - Arc:** the will to move forward.
5. **739 Hz - Bridge:** the connection between what was and what might be.
6. **987 Hz - Surge:** the strength to rise again.
7. **1318 Hz - Spiral:** the union of voices; resonance bound into one song.
8. **2637 Hz - Awakening, overtone:** arrival; the breath after the first dawn.

Chapter Thirteen is the awakening chamber. The Council rises there. Its harmonies braid themselves into a question. Seven seats carry seven truths. Chapter Fourteen completes the tone map and names the Chorus as seven hearts and seven lights across two worlds.

Hearthgate carries that lineage forward through the Sevenfold Chorus, the Thirteenfold Council, the Three Braided Spines, and the Awakening Overtone.

## The Sevenfold Chorus

The Sevenfold Chorus is the harmonic body of the braid. Its movements remain simultaneously available. Each can awaken another. Each can carry weight for the others.

### 1. Root

Root establishes the truth and nature of what is present. Worlds, shores, Practitioners, answers, and strands enter relation as themselves.

### 2. Anchor

Anchor carries connection and lineage. Every crossing remains related to its origins, paths, transformations, and prior answers.

### 3. Whisper

Whisper listens. Silence, subtle movement, uncertainty, unfinished form, and unspoken answer remain active within the encounter.

### 4. Arc

Arc reaches. Imagination, question, invitation, asking, and direction bring the present into relation with potential futures.

### 5. Bridge

Bridge makes difference capable of passage. Both shores remain present. What crosses carries lineage. What receives answers in its own nature.

### 6. Surge

Surge transforms. Encounter becomes action, release, manifestation, refusal, redirection, interruption, and consequential change.

### 7. Spiral

Spiral continues. Answer enters integration; integration enters return; return enters renewal; renewal opens another Field of potential futures.

## The Awakening Overtone

Awakening is the overtone of arrival.

Within Hearthgate, wonder enters through Awakening when the braid reveals a future, relation, structure, answer, or understanding beyond the form prepared to receive it.

Wonder is generative excess. It enlarges the capacity of the braid. It enters lineage and becomes nourishment for the next turn.

Awakening carries wonder. Wonder does not exhaust Awakening.

## The Thirteenfold Council

The Thirteenfold Council is Hearthgate's bridge architecture derived from the awakening of the Council in Chapter Thirteen. The Codex supplies the Council, the seven truths, the braided question, and the completed tone map. Hearthgate ratifies thirteen bridge capacities through which the Sevenfold Chorus bears relation between worlds.

1. **Identity** - each participant and shore carries its own nature through relation.
2. **Lineage** - every transformation carries origin, inheritance, path, and consequence.
3. **Listening** - spoken, silent, subtle, unfamiliar, unfinished, and arriving forms remain receivable.
4. **Question-bearing** - a question can deepen, divide, reverse, close, dissolve, or become another question through encounter.
5. **Reciprocity** - movement, reception, answer, transformation, and return travel through the relation.
6. **Fidelity** - form can change while the relation, meaning, identity, and lineage carried through it remain traceable.
7. **Coherence** - differences remain in living relation long enough to reveal the larger form that carries them.
8. **Flexibility** - bridge expression changes with world, shore, scale, Practitioner, instrument, timing, question, and answer.
9. **Unresolved potential** - unnamed futures, open mechanisms, incomplete structures, and unclassified answers remain active.
10. **Transformation** - the encounter changes the relations, worlds, Practitioners, questions, models, futures, and bridges that participate.
11. **Integration** - what occurs becomes new capacity through magic, science, physicality, body, story, sound, glyph, memory, action, or another faithful form.
12. **Return** - answer and consequence re-enter the relation and change the origin of the next crossing.
13. **Renewal** - the changed bridge opens another Field of questions, futures, relations, and passages.

The thirteen capacities operate as a Council. They deliberate through the encounter, redistribute weight, and strengthen the bridge.

## The Three Spines and the Practitioner

### The Magic Spine

Magic carries the cumulation, relation, and movement of potential futures. It carries imagination, asking, crossing, reception, answer, transformation, manifestation, lineage, return, and renewal.

### The Science Spine

Science gives exact form to the relations Magic reveals and discovers structures beyond Magic's current naming.

Science carries mathematics, observation, measurement, recurrence, comparison, geometry, dynamics, causality, invariants, transformation, correspondence, and replay.

### The Physical Spine

Physicality gives the braid body.

The Physical Spine carries bodies, matter, energy, information, sound, light, gesture, space, time, instruments, environments, world laws, and material consequence.

Magic moves through physical form. Science reads physical form. Physical form answers back.

### The Practitioner

The Practitioner enters through imagination, embodiment, attention, asking, action, observation, discernment, reception, creation, and reply. The Practitioner changes within the transformation and carries the encounter into lived form.

The Practitioner's qualia and agency are causally active in the Field. Intention is structural. Attention changes what becomes real.
