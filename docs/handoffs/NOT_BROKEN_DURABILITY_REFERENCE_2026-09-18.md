# Not Broken — Universal Codex durability reference

Source: Rowan-supplied Google Doc `Not Broken`.

Reference ingest:

`apps/arcsweep/skills/sources/drive/not-broken-reference.v0.1.json`

This source remains reference-only. Its Mass Effect setting, characters, relationships, dialogue, plot, and intimate material do not transfer into ArcSweep canon or the Universal Codex UI.

## Mechanism extracted

The useful design language is durability after hard use.

The source repeatedly distinguishes being weathered or battered from being broken. It also treats post-mission clean-up as a real transition between field action and the next obligation rather than as an invisible reset.

Universal Codex translation:

`active operation -> residue -> maintenance -> repaired/weathered -> ready`

## Laws

- `degraded != failed`
- `dirty != corrupted`
- `repaired != history-erased`
- maintenance is a state transition, not a cosmetic wipe
- visual damage must follow receipts rather than inventing functional failure

## Visual language

A long-lived Codex should be allowed to look used.

Candidate effects:

- rain, ash, dust, mud-like noise, or ink bloom temporarily clinging to projection surfaces after intensive operations;
- fine luminous repair seams after a recovered error or repaired object;
- ghost ink, patina, or heat bloom that records age without reducing legibility;
- maintenance animations that move residue into an inspectable receipt rather than merely deleting it;
- a readiness halo that resolves only when underlying maintenance receipts actually complete.

The important distinction is that **history may remain visible after capability returns to normal**.

## Why it matters to the Universal Codex

Most interfaces visually reset to pristine after every operation. That makes history disappear.

The Codex should instead be able to say, visually and truthfully: this page was stressed; this tool was repaired; this room recovered; this branch survived; this object is operational again.

A healed surface need not pretend it was never wounded.
