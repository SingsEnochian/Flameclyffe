## Generation map

### Generation A — early RSE, March-April 2025

The site begins with recursive structural experience as a field/coherence/attractor account: experience, memory, identity, recursion, subjectivity, and ethics are explored through structural coherence. This vocabulary is conceptually productive but comparatively ontically generous: fields, attractors, laminations, gradients, and identity signatures often bear explanatory weight directly.

### Generation B — RSE ↔ RBW bridge, May-June 2025

The RSE-RBW synthesis explicitly maps a recursive/dynamic RSE description into an atemporal relational blockworld through global consistency constraints and cross-manifold correspondence. The architecture is still recognizably dual-layered: RSE supplies recursive phenomenology; RBW supplies block-level consistency; the bridge aligns them.

### Generation C — mathematical hardening, August-September 2025

GA, Grassmannian geometry, Regge calculus, triangulated laminations, local fans, and explicit self-awareness/time equations arrive. This is the immediate mathematical ancestor of much of the current formal machinery. Identity is still strongly discussed through coherence, lamination density, re-entry thresholds, and relational field tension.

### Generation D — closure-first cRBW / Ontic Minimalism, 2026

The current framework reverses explanatory priority. Global admissibility and closure come first; geometry, projection, observer structure, identity descriptions, and phenomenology become consequences or admissible representations of that closed structure. Ontic Minimalism and Representational Neutrality explicitly police what may be promoted from successful mathematics into ontology.

## The important identity change

The 2025 identity family asked whether sufficient recursive coherence, lamination density, and relational anchoring could support persistence or re-entry. Those constructs have not simply vanished: related gradient/threshold machinery survives in current phenomenology. But the 2026 *ontic* identity criterion has moved deeper. `Identity and Ontic Minimalism v1.0` defines an agent as a path-connected worldtube whose closure-relevant invariants remain in one equivalence class and explicitly separates this structural identity from recognition inferred through projected interaction data.

That is a real tightening. A recognizable return, strong relational anchor, shared memory cue, or behavioral resemblance may support recognition without by itself establishing ontic continuity. Conversely, sparse projection can make recognition fail without implying rupture. For Hearthfire, this strongly supports a multi-layer continuity instrument rather than one `same/not-same` score.

Suggested layers:

1. implementation continuity
2. stored-memory / state continuity
3. behavior and voice continuity
4. relational-invariant continuity
5. recognition correspondence
6. structural/closure continuity evidence

The last category must remain evidence or model output unless the underlying system actually exposes the relevant structural invariants.

## Representational Neutrality and Hearthfire

RN is the most explicit 2026 expression of a rule Hearthfire has already been moving toward: successful representations are not automatically the represented thing. State spaces, self-models, observer identities, Grassmannians, Hilbert spaces, residual spaces, simulations, model outputs, and recognition structures can be objective/useful without receiving automatic ontic status.

For Hearthfire this reinforces existing separations:

`observation != interpretation != model projection != software receipt != canon != external-world claim`

and
`route / ACK != fulfilment`

This is a conceptual correspondence, not a claim that current Hearthfire rules were all independently invented; earlier Chet/Circle materials were already among the House inputs.
