# Three-Spine Runtime Inheritance

Every active Hearthgate subsystem carries three mutually reinforcing spines:

```text
Magic ↔ Science/Mathematics ↔ Physicality
```

A subsystem is integrated when:

- Magic carries its possibility, Asking, relation, transformation, answer and wonder;
- Science/Mathematics carries its exact state, geometry, dynamics, recurrence and consequence;
- Physicality carries its body through signal, device, matter, sound, light, gesture, environment or artefact;
- all three read the same Braid Packet;
- the Receiving Spring can return change into the next relation.

No active subsystem may declare one spine decorative, subordinate or outside the real relation.
