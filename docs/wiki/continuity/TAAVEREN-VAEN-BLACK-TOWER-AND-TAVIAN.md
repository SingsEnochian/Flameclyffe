# Ta’veren Vaen: The Fourth Age Black Tower and Tavian Corren

**Status:** In Review  
**World:** Ta’veren Vaen  
**Era:** Fourth Age, approximately two thousand years after the Last Battle  
**Arcsweep bundle:** `2026.08.11.14`

## Canon declarations

- Saidin has remained clean since Rand al’Thor cleansed it before the Last Battle.
- The Black Tower survived and developed into a sovereign civic and channelling institution.
- It is neither subordinate to the White Tower nor confined to military purpose.
- Asha’man is the fully earned standing of guardian and servant of all.
- The historical title M’Hael is retained only in condemnation of Mazrim Taim and coercive rule.
- White and Black Towers cooperate without merging.
- Resonant Bonding belongs exclusively to neither Tower.

## Institutional memory

The Black Tower remembers liberation and betrayal together. Rand ordered men who could channel to be found and trained. Logain Ablar helped break the Shadow’s seizure of the Tower. Men were forcibly Turned within its walls. These truths are taught together because triumph without the crime would make the crime repeatable.

Families and non-channellers were present from the founding generations. Partners, children, builders, healers, farmers, clerks, teachers, refugees, and craftspeople converted a Last Battle camp into a community capable of surviving peace.

## Present structure

The founding ground remains an ancestral seat and archive. Regional houses operate near major roads, rivers, borders, industrial centres, and dangerous remnant sites.

A term-limited First Asha’man governs beside a deliberative council representing regional houses, disciplines, archives, teachers, and the resident non-channelling community. Recruitment, discipline, field command, treaties, and archives cannot be concentrated under one person.

Training begins with safe seizure and release of saidin, bodily regulation, mathematics, history, law, first aid, ordinary tools, and work performed without the Power. Students study the Tower’s betrayals as carefully as its victories.

Recognised paths include protection and defence; Healing and living systems; construction and structural rescue; weather and land; gateways and logistics; scholarship and remnants; civic craft and industry; diplomacy and mixed-circle practice.

The structural-rescue path teaches one central law:

> Holding is not the same as saving.

## Relations with Tar Valon

The Towers recognise one another without claiming completion or ownership. They maintain embassies, archive exchanges, teaching visits, disputed jurisdictions, and joint field compacts. Leadership in a mixed circle follows the work and may transfer during an operation.

Yellow healers and Black Tower practitioners exchange medical knowledge. Green field companies work regularly beside Asha’man. Brown sisters and remnant scholars dispute custody and interpretation. Red sisters have evolved into investigators of coercive channelling and Power crime across gender.

## Tavian Corren

Tavian Corren is twenty-four and a fully raised Asha’man of Greyspan House. Construction and structural rescue are his primary path; mixed-circle field practice is his supporting discipline.

He was raised among bridges, scaffolds, steam pumps, barges, foundries, mills, and floods. His family’s trades taught him load, grain, current, heat, leverage, and the sound of material approaching failure. The Black Tower gave mathematical and channelling language to knowledge his hands already carried.

Earth and Fire are his strongest elements, followed by Spirit and Water. Air is least natural. His Talent detects stress, resonance, and imminent failure in worked structures, active weaves, Power-made objects, and places altered by catastrophe.

He speaks quietly and exactly. His humour is dry, badly timed, and most likely to surface while something is burning. He distrusts the word heroic when it is used to sanctify preventable sacrifice.

Three years before meeting Kestrelle, he held the failing span of an industrial river crossing beyond its declared release threshold. Most of the final rescue team escaped; two people died. The collapse injured his left side and burned a resonance through his nervous system. He retains weakness and tremor in his left hand, pain through his shoulder and ribs, and disorientation under dense vibration.

Healing saved him. It did not erase what happened.

Greyspan returned him to field service with explicit thresholds, instrumentation, a second structural lead, and mandatory transfer conditions. He follows these rules while still carrying the belief that another person’s survival is worth more than his continued wholeness.

## First convergence with Kestrelle

Tavian and Kestrelle meet at **Harrowspan Wayhouse** on the River Miren, between the mill village of Bellfound and the western market roads. The inhabited complex includes an inn, stable yard, toll office, repair shed, river landing, garden, and road-treatment room.

The present Miren Bridge contains three eras of construction: ancient Power-made footings, later stone arches, and a forty-six-year-old riveted iron centre. Its waking load travels through five effective bays. In Tel’aran’rhiod, it remains a complete three-arch stone bridge beneath nine lit lanterns.

### The Nine-Lantern Collapse

Forty-six years earlier, floodwater undermined the old centre pier while residents, workers, guests, and travellers were crossing at night. Eleven people died. An unknown channeller held the bridge's last coherent shape through its buried footings while a rope line cleared survivors from both banks—but never completed the weave's release.

The waking bridge collapsed. Its Dream reflection remained fixed at the final moment when rescue was still possible.

The later iron bridge is mechanically sound under ordinary conditions, but floods, heavy crossings, and channelled repairs excite the buried instruction. The bridge then tries to become the remembered shape in which it was held. Rivets loosen in groups of three, six, and nine; survey marks migrate; repaired stones move towards the geometry of a pier that no longer exists.

Recent dredging and a steam traction engine with two millstone wagons have driven the system into resonance. The iron span is now receiving force from its absent Dream pier.

Tavian maps every physical and woven failure but cannot explain why repaired stones return to their broken positions. Kestrelle hears the place insisting upon its remembered shape but cannot keep the waking structure standing.

Neither is mistaken. Neither is sufficient.

The place tells Kestrelle: **Not yet; they are not all across.**

The unfinished holding weave must be witnessed, released, and translated into a new load path belonging to the living bridge. Matter must lead first; memory must lead last.

### First-response compact

- Tavian leads first, establishing a temporary five-bay waking load path.
- Three low strikes announce the transfer window.
- Kestrelle accepts leadership with: **I have the far bank.**
- She carries the circle through Spirit and Earth into the remembered instruction.
- The release phrase is: **All living souls are across.**
- Either may end the circle on **Feather**.
- Transfer becomes mandatory if Tavian's tremor prevents two consecutive instrument readings.

The scene's immediate human constraint is an injured mill worker who cannot survive rough transport while floodwater blocks the south road. The bridge is not an abstract puzzle; people still need it.

## Relationship trajectory

Their romance is canon. It develops through fieldwork, argument, humour, repair, reciprocal teaching, and the increasingly obvious fact that each has begun designing safer ways for the other to return.

They eventually enter a Resonant Bonding: one jointly made saidar–saidin weave preserving two complete people in durable harmonic correspondence. Neither becomes Warder, owner, commander, or subordinate to the other.
