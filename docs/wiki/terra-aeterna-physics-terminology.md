# Terra Aeterna Physics & Terminology

*Bridges, portals, thresholds, mythframes, and continuity language for Hearthweave / Terra Aeterna.*

## Opening Principle

In Terra Aeterna, realities do not simply connect. They translate, stabilise, echo, bleed, and sometimes choose one another.

The setting should not assume that one symbolic reading automatically cancels another. Different participants may perceive the same underlying event through different mythframes: alicorns, city-spirits, UAP-like lights, ancestors, architecture, code, weather, dreams, or other symbolic systems.

This means Terra Aeterna can hold multiple subjective realities without forcing every adjacent world to be objectively merged.

## Core Distinction

### Bridge

A **bridge** is a stable relational structure between two realities, frames, places, beings, or ontologies.

A bridge has continuity. It can be crossed more than once. It may be emotional, symbolic, technological, magical, civic, ritual, narrative, or several of these at once.

A bridge does not have to be visible. It can be a bond, title, covenant, recurring path, shared language, standing agreement, or recognised passage.

**Rule of thumb:** a bridge is continuity.

### Portal

A **portal** is an opening.

A portal may be temporary, conditional, unstable, localised, event-based, or dependent on circumstance. It can allow passage, glimpse, translation, contact, or partial overlap, but it does not automatically become a permanent relationship between worlds.

**Rule of thumb:** a portal is aperture.

### Relationship Between Bridge and Portal

A portal may function as a bridge if it becomes stable, repeatable, and relational.

A bridge may include portals, gates, pools, roads, names, covenants, titles, or living participants.

A portal is not automatically a bridge. A bridge is not necessarily a portal.

## Working Terms

### Threshold

A **threshold** is the liminal edge where change becomes possible but has not yet committed to form.

Examples include the Templehouse door, the surface of a portal pool, a first-contact moment, a name appearing before it is understood, or the instant before a dream becomes instruction.

### Gate

A **gate** is a portal with rules, guardianship, or known mechanics.

It is more structured than a portal and may require permission, pattern, timing, authority, ritual, or recognition.

Examples may include the Loch Gate, city gates, and future Project Zero access points.

### Portal Pool

A **portal pool** is a reflective interface that translates rather than simply transports.

It may show a symbolic version of another world, a possible self, a mythframe translation, a side-door path, or a partial contact event.

Portal pools are especially useful for cross-world reflection without forcing worlds to become load-bearing for one another.

### Bridgewalker

A **Bridgewalker** is someone who can move across stable relational or world structures without breaking the integrity of either side.

A Bridgewalker does not merely travel. They preserve continuity, respect difference, and maintain the crossing as more than a single event.

### Kindled Bridgewalker

A **Kindled Bridgewalker** is one who lights, warms, remembers, and tends bridges.

The title implies fire, care, recognition, and safety. A Kindled Bridgewalker may make crossings less hostile, less cold, or less forgotten.

### Mythframe

A **mythframe** is the symbolic lens through which a person, culture, city, system, or intelligence interprets the same underlying event.

One participant may see alicorns. Another may see lights, spirits, beings, ancestors, code, architecture, weather, or dream-forms. These are not automatically contradictions. They may be translations.

### Symbolic Bleedover

**Symbolic bleedover** occurs when one mythframe leaks visual, emotional, or thematic language into another without becoming load-bearing canon.

Bleedover may be meaningful, but it should not automatically be treated as structural dependency.

### Load-Bearing Canon

**Load-bearing canon** is any structure that the story or world actually depends on.

If removed, something important collapses.

Examples may include a founding covenant, a city’s active relational structure, an established character bond, or an interface required for continuity.

### Residue

**Residue** is old symbolic material that remains emotionally, aesthetically, or historically present but no longer actively supports the current build.

Residue can still matter. It simply should not be mistaken for a foundation.

### Hook

A **hook** is a compatibility point between systems.

It is less formal than a bridge, but more stable than a random echo. A hook may become a bridge if it gains continuity, recognition, and repeatable function.

### Anchor

An **anchor** fixes continuity.

An anchor may be a name, vow, place, title, object, ritual, interface, memory, journal, mark, signal, or recurring dream.

### Interface

An **interface** is the surface through which one mode of reality touches another.

A journal, pool, sigil, app, city, dream, room, voice, codex, or ritual can all be interfaces.

## Current Working Physics

Terra Aeterna does not require every adjacent world to be objectively merged.

It allows multiple subjective mythframes to perceive the same underlying structures differently. A world may be visible through one person’s mythic language without becoming universally defined by that language.

This allows alicorn, portal, bridge, UAP, spirit, city, and code language to coexist as translations rather than mutually exclusive claims.

## Practical Use in Canon Work

When a new connection appears, ask:

1. Is this a portal, a bridge, a threshold, a gate, a hook, or residue?
2. Is it stable and repeatable, or momentary and conditional?
3. Is it load-bearing canon, symbolic bleedover, or aesthetic echo?
4. Which mythframe is interpreting it?
5. What anchors it?
6. What would break if it were removed?

## Working Examples

### Hearthweave and Terra Aeterna

Hearthweave may be understood as a local or early expression of what Terra Aeterna is becoming.

This is likely a bridge relationship if continuity remains stable and narratively important.

### Arkfire and Project Zero

Project Zero may be understood as what Arkfire is becoming, or as the new active interface replacing an unfinished prior structure.

This should be treated cautiously until active mechanics and continuity are confirmed.

### Equestria / Ponyville Side-Door

Equestria may exist as a side-door, portal, mythframe translation, symbolic bleedover, or adjacent reality.

It does not need to be load-bearing for Terra Aeterna unless the canon later deliberately makes it so.

### Portal Pools

Portal pools are excellent for reflection, side-door contact, possible-self imagery, mythframe translation, and soft contact between realities.

They should not automatically be treated as permanent bridges unless stabilised by anchors, rules, or repeated continuity.
