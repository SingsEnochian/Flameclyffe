## Domain declaration

Each statement carries one domain:

```text
ENGINE-TRUE
OBSERVATION-TRUE
CANON-TRUE
SYMBOLIC
UNKNOWN
REJECTED
```

`ENGINE-TRUE` means the statement is an axiom, invariant, operator or accepted state transition of Hearthgate.

`OBSERVATION-TRUE` means the statement is present in an evidence-grounded current-reality source packet.

`CANON-TRUE` means the statement is established by the registered canon foundation or authored overlay within its declared world.

`SYMBOLIC` means the quantity remains inside the mathematics as a variable, interval, distribution or admissible set.

`UNKNOWN` means the source packet contains no value. The engine preserves the open variable and continues every operation that does not require an exact value.

`REJECTED` means the statement failed its declared acceptance gate.

Domains never overwrite one another.
