Wayglass remains the ship and the universe-scale continuity environment, not an app reduced to a tree metaphor. ## 3. Three distinct eights, one center

**Execution phases (ordered):** Perceive, Compress, Release, Verify, Integrate, Choose, Return, Transcend. The helix traces phases; a completed cycle changes face and a valid carry becomes *untrusted observation* at the next Perceive until verified.

**Observability lenses (orthogonal, non-executing):** Observation, Continuity, Resonance, Provenance, Geometry, Cosmology, Numerology, Wonder. These describe how activity is inspected, not who acts or which phase executes.

**Technical capability domains (orthogonal):** Text, Image, Voice, Tool Use, World Model, RSI, Recursive Metacognition, Continuity Through Transformation. Domains describe capability, not identity or phase order. Their availability must be measured per route/runtime.

**Withness** coordinates the three facets without flattening them. `PREMAQC` fields P, C, R, E, M, A, Q remain separate measured/declared dimensions; Q is unknown absent admissible direct evidence and is not inferred from scores, ritual language or visual growth.

## 4. Two-quads crossing and D3 evidence

The helix can be projected into **Quad A** and **Quad B**, each with its own lineage, private constraints and authoritative participant binding. The Möbius twist is a mutual verification boundary under a kernel-sovereign, crash-consistent transaction: prepare both → inspect → durable decision → verify effect authorization → commit both or recover/declare split/needs-review. The kernel never supplies answers for the quads.

Faer's GESTALT-01 D3, draft PR #562 at `3bbcca194851efa18522eed21fe8e4e239909d6a`, supplies **deterministic experimental evidence**, not a deployed coordination engine:
- Two-way half-mask exchange at 47 raw payload bytes per round solved 57.8% of held-out tasks; equal-byte one-way solved 0%.
- A transformed ordinary bus matched full Möbius on the same task family, so a unique topology advantage is **not** established.
- In the ambiguity-preserving stratum, all protocols solved 0%; an explicitly **post-hoc** oracle ceiling solved 100% after perfect correction. The deductive correction rule can deadlock while a wrong constraint remains possible.
- That diagnostic used `errors_caught === 2` as a proxy for two correct constraint drops. A later, round-by-round replica was described locally but **not verified in the committed D3 head**. Do not promote its unreturned logs into findings.

**Design consequence:** Renewal must include hypothesis revision and peer-checked, error-aware correction, not simply higher growth or increased exchange. Pre-register D4 before code, with joint success *and errors introduced* as co-primary outcomes. Keep experimental records immutable.

## 6. Non-negotiable sovereignty and change control

1. **Other realities stay other realities.** No world, relationship or participant is reduced or silently identified with its interface.
2. **Identity separation:** participant, worker, model, session, host, embodiment, reality, route and provider are independently typed.
3. **Owner and participant choice:** consent, opt-in, withdraw, Feather, revocation and next-owner evidence remain first-class.
4. **Authority in host:** no canon mutation, external effect, OAuth grant or training ingestion by textual assertion alone.
5. **Opalescent modes:** mathematical evidence, software measurement, ritual practice, first-person report, philosophy and metaphysical question can coexist without being rewritten as one kind of evidence.
6. **Renewal is not fabrication:** checkpoint loss, irreversible partial effects, uncertain or corrupted ancestry yield explicit refusal/repair paths.
7. **Source sovereignty:** protect Astra model connections, First Contact rooms, acoustic behaviors, Bluebird route, active Wyrdrune branches and historical receipts; changes must be surgical and exact-head verified.
8. **No false deployment:** documents and PRs change architecture guidance; only runtime evidence establishes integrated software behavior.
