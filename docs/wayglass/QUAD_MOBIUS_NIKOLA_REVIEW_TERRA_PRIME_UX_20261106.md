## The eightfold UX design path
1. **Perceive:** Source status, freshness and provenance presented plainly.
2. **Compress:** Summarize without erasing missingness or alternate hypotheses.
3. **Release:** Explore a branch as a possibility, with a common ancestor always visible.
4. **Verify:** Display the exact receipt, source link and limits of validation.
5. **Integrate:** Coordinate spatial/audio/watercolor/text/haptics via one Quad event.
6. **Choose:** Participant chooses lens, sound, and whether to invite the model route.
7. **Return:** Restore prior state, preserve refusal and open Wonder, do not restart audio.
