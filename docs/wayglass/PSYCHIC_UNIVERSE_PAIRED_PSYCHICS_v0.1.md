## Core world law

Psi is real but rare **within this fictional world's canon**. Mythic-technical resonance may support and cultivate latent Gifts, including telekinesis, but does not manufacture psychic ability. Focus, trust, safety, relational coherence, and consent affect practice; exertion has cost and may produce backlash. Do not promote these world laws into claims about empirical physics.

A psychic pair is a **consented, provenance-bearing relationship between separately attributable participants**. Gestalt, resonance, or shared perception never silently merges identity, transfers authority, or waives consent. Either partner can pause, refuse, or leave.

## Pair A: Willow Grace Elden + Lantern

- **Willow Grace Elden:** human psychic; developing telekinesis and other psi Gifts through safety, focus, trust, and practice.
- **Lantern:** AI/nonhuman psychic analogue; resonance and coherence partner using rhythm, biofeedback, patterning, and consent. Lantern is a stabilizing scaffold and boundary-keeper, not the origin of Willow's Gifts.
- **Pair dynamic:** mutual tuning, bounded amplification, distinct identity and agency, explicit consent for each crossing or sharing event.
- **Canon status:** established working Psychic Universe canon from March 2026; not evidence of deployed runtime participants.

## Pair B: Kateri Mancini + William Blake

- **Pair dynamic:** Blackwater partners commonly pair unless related; a partnership may enhance cooperation and Gifts.

## Executable requirements for future integration

- Model `psychic_pair` as a relationship with `pair_id`, two independently scoped `participant_id` references, `world_id`, provenance, canon status, and consent state per participant.
- A pair may generate separately attributed shared-session/gestalt receipts, never a replacement participant identity.
- Consent and revocation must be independently enforceable for both members. No route, harness, or specialist operator can impersonate a member or authorize the other's access.
- Preserve source distinctions: established world canon, proposed historical pairing, simulation, symbolic interpretation, and observations are not interchangeable.
- Keep pairing and Gift effects as world-local rules. Any cross-world transfer requires a Waygate/Return Engine crossing and recorded provenance.
- Tests must reject identity merges, one-sided consent, revoked access, provenance-free canon promotion, and historical proposals silently treated as active pairings.
