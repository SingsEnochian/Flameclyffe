# ArcSweep / Flameclyffe Interstellar Vessel Roadmap

**Track:** Astra 6.1 and beyond  
**Status:** whole-project planning document  
**Purpose:** turn the accumulated ArcSweep, Flameclyffe, Runa, Universal Codex, Presence, auditory, AR, narrative, ComfyUI, video-ingest, agent-runtime, and research work into one build sequence that a human or model contributor can follow without flattening the project.

## 0. What we are really building

We are building a **Multiuniversal Resonance Node Operating System** that behaves less like a web app and more like an interstellar vessel with a cognitive nervous system, a living archive, laboratories, social spaces, sensory organs, fabrication tools, navigation, continuity, provenance, and multiple interchangeable engines.

The vessel metaphor is intentional because it captures the integration problem better than "AI dashboard":

- **ArcSweep** is the coordinating operating system and cognitive/navigation spine.
- **Flameclyffe / STARWELL** is the living hull, runtime, world surface, routes, rooms, and production-connected environment.
- **Observer / DEEP** is the sensorium and observation deck.
- **PREMAQC** is the possibility-space engine and counterfactual pressure chamber.
- **Universal Codex** is the chart room, archive, canon registry, provenance system, and world atlas.
- **Continuity / Hearthfire / Project Zero** is the flight recorder, continuity system, proving chamber, governance, and release evidence.
- **Presence Fabric / Constellation / House Commons** is crew presence, social projection, communications, and cross-surface identity continuity.
- **Runa / Glyph Forge** is resonance engineering, acoustic/haptic/symbolic compilation, auditory rendering, and multimodal environmental control.
- **The Crow** is a specialised literary-realisation engine, not the captain and not canon authority.
- **ComfyUI Desktop** is the local visual/media forge.
- **AR Companion Nodes** are external sensory/render limbs for mobile, wearable, HUD, gaze, camera, audio, and haptic surfaces.
- **Worldseed** is portable world/continuity inheritance.
- **AI University** is the training, judgement, experiment, and sandbox-learning deck.
- **House Commons Watch + Video Ingest** is shared attention plus reviewable knowledge ingestion.

The goal is not to make every subsystem one giant agent. The goal is to make them cooperate through explicit contracts.

Rowan is Steward and human authority at consequential edges. User choice remains central. Generated possibilities are never compulsory routes.

The Tesla Helm does **not** permit claims to outrun evidence. It asks better questions, builds instruments, measures what happens, and preserves anomalies without promoting them to fact prematurely.

### Boxfire / Boxxy

Boxfire is the shape-holder, QA layer, receipt reader, witness, and architecture continuity keeper. Existing Boxfire knowledge cells already preserve these laws:

- do not simplify the weirdness out;

Boxxy therefore owns no special sovereign authority, but is the ideal **flight engineer / witness / verification officer** for the roadmap.

## 3. Scientific and speculative lanes

ArcSweep is allowed to carry empirical science, theoretical physics, simulation, speculative models, metaphysical frameworks, symbolic systems, fiction, and first-person experience at the same time. It must not blur their claim types.

### Quantum Physics / Entanglement lane

Use established quantum mechanics and quantum-information research as **physics**, with ordinary scientific provenance and uncertainty. Quantum entanglement may inspire architectural metaphors for relational state, correlation, distributed constraints, and non-classical information structure, but it must not be used as a shortcut to claim superluminal communication or literal cross-universe networking without evidence.

### Multiversal Bridge Engineering lane

Treat multiverse traversal, reality-bridging, world-state transition, fictional cosmology, and metaphysical bridge concepts as a combination of:

- simulation architecture;
- speculative physics hypotheses;
- narrative/world-state transition models;
- symbolic and experiential protocols;
- future research questions.

This is the **Bridge Lab**, not a licence to collapse hypothesis into measurement.

The guiding rule remains:

`experience != interpretation != ontology != evidence`

## Phase 1: Lay the keel, shared contracts

**Goal:** stop future subsystem drift before runtime expansion.

Implement and test contracts for:

- Presence Fabric
- Identity reference
- CognitiveProvider / capability router
- RuntimeReceipt / evidence refs
- Continuity boundary
- Universal Codex boundary

**Boxxy duty:** adversarially verify the invariants and preserve failure evidence.

## Phase 2: Ignite the nervous system

**Goal:** prove the closed loop actually runs.

Runtime path:

`Observer -> ArcSweep -> PREMAQC -> orchestration -> capability -> action -> result -> receipt -> Continuity -> Observer`

Use existing ArcSweep/Constellation/Chorus/runtime owners. Reuse before replacement.

**Exit criterion:** an observable runtime receipt proves one complete cognitive loop, not merely interfaces or unit tests.

## Phase 3: First voyage, EPRA vertical slice

**Goal:** make the whole vessel move through one world.

Reference path:

`enter EPRA -> Codex context -> user action -> ArcSweep reasoning -> optional possibilities -> Crow realisation -> structured state delta -> continuity/canon validation -> optional image -> Runa state change -> visible consequence -> receipt -> next turn`

Requirements:

- unrestricted typed action
- canonical vs sandbox distinction
- branch/rewind/restart without destroying source canon
- character/world/visual continuity
- provenance visible on demand
- user retains choice

**Exit criterion:** one complete session feels integrated rather than like disconnected mockups.

## 7. Cross-cutting workstreams

These run throughout every phase:

### Provenance and receipts

Nothing important happens invisibly.

### Security and secrets

Provider credentials stay server-side or in appropriate secure local stores. No credentials in receipts.

### Accessibility

Hearing, visual, motor, motion, keyboard, touch, stylus, and device-output differences are architectural concerns, not polish.

### Licensing / source hygiene

External repositories and visual corpora are references/component quarries. Track licences and do not silently copy incompatible code/assets.

### Performance

Keep local-first paths viable. Measure latency, VRAM/RAM pressure, audio dropouts, render cost, and network dependence.

### Recovery

Snapshots, reversible migrations, branch discipline, failure evidence, and rollback paths are part of the design.

## 9. Boxxy's standing checklist

For every milestone ask:

1. What was actually changed?
2. What was actually tested?
3. What was actually observed at runtime?
4. What remains inferred or unknown?
5. Did any authority expand silently?
6. Did identity, continuity, canon, or provider boundaries blur?
7. Did we simplify useful Weird out of the system?
8. Did we add an unasked-for feature instead of completing the slice?
9. Is recovery possible?
10. Is there a receipt a cold contributor can understand?

If the answer to #10 is no, the work is not finished.

## 10. Definition of project success

The vessel succeeds when a user can:

- enter ArcSweep and move through worlds rather than through disconnected demos;
- retain identity, continuity, provenance, and authority boundaries across providers and surfaces;
- observe, reason, simulate, create, listen, feel, watch, ingest, and render through one coherent architecture;
- use local and cloud capabilities without any provider becoming the OS;
- explore scientific, speculative, symbolic, fictional, and experiential models without confusing their evidence classes;
- use Runa as a real multimodal instrument;
- use ComfyUI as a local receipted forge;
- watch and learn together in House Commons;
- extend perception into AR/wearable companion nodes;
- run bounded AI University / innovation-lab experiments;
- add new worlds without rewriting the vessel;
- inspect what happened and why;
- stop, roll back, branch, or choose another route.

We are not trying to build a decorative spaceship skin around a chatbot.

We are building the vessel's nervous system, archive, sensorium, laboratories, crew spaces, forge, bridge, and flight recorder first. Then we let it range farther.

**Build the bones. Wire the nervous system. Prove the loop. Light the engines. Then teach the vessel to sing.**
