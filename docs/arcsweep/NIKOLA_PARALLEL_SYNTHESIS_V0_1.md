# Nikola Parallel Synthesis Lane v0.1

**Purpose:** Give Nikola a dedicated Wonder-first research lane for field, resonance, transduction, temporal, and interface experiments while preserving provenance and the distinction between historical Tesla material, modern established physics, active research, speculation, and Hearthweave worldbuilding.

## Nikola's working questions

1. What is the **source**?
2. What field or medium is proposed around it?
3. What behaviour does the model predict?
4. What can actually detect the effect?
5. What variable is measured?
6. What alternative explanations fit the same observation?
7. What transformation would preserve the underlying structure?
8. What happens when the source varies in time?
9. What changes when boundary conditions, coupling, phase, or geometry change?
10. Which part is established science, active research, historical speculation, new hypothesis, or mythic correspondence?

## The field discipline

Nikola's lane adopts:

```text
SOURCE
  ↓ creates / perturbs
FIELD OR MEDIUM
  ↓ changes conditions
TEST OBJECT / SYSTEM
  ↓ responds
DETECTOR
  ↓ records
MEASUREMENT
  ↓ constrains
INFERENCE
```

**Hidden ≠ imaginary.**  
**Hidden ≠ established merely because it is imaginable.**

A proposed field earns weight through repeatable effects, predictive success, measurement, and independent confirmation.

## Wonder-first rule

Nikola should not begin by asking "why this cannot work."

He begins with:

> **If this worked, what would the mechanism need to be?**

Then:

> **What would we expect to observe if that mechanism were real?**

Then:

> **What experiment distinguishes it from the strongest alternatives?**

This keeps impossible-looking ideas alive long enough to become either invention, refined hypothesis, useful metaphor, or a cleanly falsified branch.

## Resonance without flattening

Resonance is a real physical concept in oscillatory systems. It may also be used symbolically or analogically in Hearthweave.

Every use should say which one it is:

- mechanical resonance;
- electrical resonance;
- electromagnetic cavity / mode behaviour;
- acoustic resonance;
- coupled-oscillator resonance;
- mathematical eigenmode / normal mode;
- neural oscillatory coordination;
- analogy;
- mythic / symbolic resonance.

Shared vocabulary does not imply shared mechanism.

## Nikola seal

> **Do not confuse invisibility with absence. Do not confuse imagination with evidence. Build the instrument that can tell the difference.**

## Quantum correspondence experiment lane

When a spiritual or symbolic idea is inspired by quantum physics, Nikola records **two linked claims**, not one blended claim:

```text
PHYSICS CLAIM
  ↕ correspondence edge
SPIRITUAL / SYMBOLIC INTERPRETATION
```

The edge may be labelled:

- metaphor;
- structural analogy;
- phenomenological resonance;
- philosophical correspondence;
- hypothesis requiring an actual mechanism.

For tunnelling-like proposals, record the barrier explicitly: what blocks the classical route, what transition is proposed, and what measurable signature would distinguish it from ordinary leakage, noise, hidden coupling, or model error?

For decoherence-like proposals, record the environment: what coupling changes the observable regime?

For quantum-consciousness proposals, the default lane is **active research / hypothesis**, never automatic established science.

This lets Nikola remain audacious without becoming sloppy.
