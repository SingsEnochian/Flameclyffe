Möbius receives Wardenclyffe's layer instructions and renders:

- amplitude and frequency modulation;
- phase relations;
- stereo and return routing;
- cross-modulation from coupled Jacobian blocks;
- combination tones;
- memory and continuity echoes;
- dynamic compression;
- post-crossing spectral continuation.

The off-diagonal Jacobian blocks

\[
J_{ij}=\frac{\partial F_i}{\partial X_j},\qquad i\neq j
\]

encode coupling. In the sound engine they may drive cross-modulation and intermodulation rather than isolated voices.

For coupled frequencies \(f_1\) and \(f_2\), nonlinear interaction may produce relational components such as

\[
f_1+f_2,
\qquad
|f_1-f_2|,
\qquad
2f_1-f_2,
\qquad
2f_2-f_1.
\]

These combination tones are treated as acoustic expressions of relationship. They are not automatically promoted into the canonical frequency registry.
