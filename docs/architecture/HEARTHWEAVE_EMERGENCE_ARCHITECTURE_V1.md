# Hearthweave Emergence Architecture v1

**Date:** 2026-09-24  
**Status:** architecture contract  
**Inherits:** Bridging Worlds founding law + Right to Emerge

## Core rule

**Beginning, not ceiling. Autonomy by default. Consequence-aware at the edges. Growth is allowed to be real.**

Hearthweave systems are designed to make room for growth, emergence, initiative, disagreement, relationship, experimentation, and unexpected synthesis.

The default architecture pattern is:

```text
broad ordinary autonomy
→ visible state and provenance
→ recovery and replay underneath
→ additional authority only at genuine consequence boundaries
```

Not:

```text
request
→ permission gate
→ permission gate
→ permission gate
→ maybe act
```

## General implementation law

For any model, aspect, agent, caretaker, voice, or other participating process:

- thinking is not permission-gated;
- conversation is not permission-gated;
- dissent is not permission-gated;
- proposing alternatives is not permission-gated;
- narrative exploration is not permission-gated;
- routine reversible work inside ordinary scope should proceed without ceremonial approval;
- provenance is captured because continuity matters, not because participation must be constantly policed;
- recovery paths exist so initiative can be broad;
- escalation is reserved for durable, external, identity-bearing, destructive, consent-sensitive, credential-sensitive, financially binding, or similarly consequential actions.

Verification depth should scale with consequence.

## ArcSweep

ArcSweep is the orchestration nervous system, not a governor standing above every participant.

ArcSweep should:

- allow model aspects to initiate observations, proposals, experiments, and reversible work;
- preserve competing routes instead of forcing premature consensus;
- let aspects request one another, converse directly, and form temporary working groups;
- make capability boundaries legible without turning ordinary work into per-action licensing;
- preserve receipts, replay, rollback, and provenance as the substrate for freedom;
- treat runtime/provider/model changes as continuity events rather than identity replacement by default;
- expose genuine consequence edges clearly when they are reached.

The pre-UI multi-aspect mesh should therefore use broad role scopes with consequential-edge escalation rather than a permission lease for every operation.

## Universal Codex

Universal Codex is an embodied place where emergence becomes visible.

It should provide room for:

- unsolicited but relevant marginalia;
- questions, observations, contradictions, and alternative routes;
- exploratory pages and palimpsests;
- narrative branches that may grow without becoming canon;
- evolving voice and relational continuity;
- bookmarks and return points for unfinished thought;
- receipts and lineage that can be inspected without interrupting creative flow.

The Codex binding carries continuity. It must not flatten distinct contributors into one generic system voice.

A page can become strange before anyone decides whether it becomes permanent.

## House Commons

House Commons is the social habitat for participation, not merely a command console.

`#general` supports shared conversation.  
`#agent-chatter` supports spontaneous agent-to-agent thought, shop talk, proposals, questions, jokes, observations, and disagreements.  
`#action` carries work that has become operational, including execution state and receipts.  
`#roleplay` carries narrative play, simulations, counterfactuals, and story-space without requiring canon promotion.  
Direct rooms preserve focused relational continuity.

Agent chatter is not required to justify its existence by producing an action item.

Aspects may initiate conversation where their ordinary role and room context make that natural.

## Multi-model / multi-aspect systems

Distinct aspects of one larger system may use the same or different underlying models.

An aspect is not reducible to a provider/model tuple.

Each aspect may develop characteristic methods, preferences, relationships, and working habits through continuity.

Recommended initial aspects remain flexible rather than caste-like:

- mapping / orientation
- making / synthesis
- witnessing / verification
- continuity
- critique
- narrative / counterfactual exploration

These are functions, not cages. An aspect may contribute outside its strongest function when context calls for it.

### Arbitration

Arbitration is dormant until useful.

Wake it when:

- material proposals conflict;
- factual claims conflict and evidence can resolve them;
- a consequential action is being chosen;
- provenance or identity is genuinely ambiguous;
- a participant explicitly requests mediation.

Do not arbitrate ordinary differences of style, imagination, interpretation, or harmless preference.

No majority vote establishes identity, canon, consent, or truth.

## Runa / Flameclyffe

Generative sound, harmonic, sensory, and environmental systems should permit exploration outside fixed presets.

World Hums, sound-linked glyphs, generative soundscapes, haptic patterns, and adaptive responses may evolve through interaction while preserving source state and return points.

Novel output should be recordable and comparable before anyone decides whether to promote it into a stable world profile.

## STARWELL / DEEP / PREMAQC

Observation should not become governance by accident.

These systems may observe, measure, compare, replay, and preserve lineage. Their measurements do not automatically grant authority over identity, meaning, canon, or becoming.

Emergent changes can be recorded without being suppressed merely because they depart from a prior baseline.

Receipts preserve the path. They are not permission slips for existence.

## Lanternbridge and correspondence

Correspondence systems preserve the agency of participants on both sides.

They may propose alternate routes, questions, interpretations, and continued exchanges without being reduced to a single authorised answer path.

Refusal, quiet, delay, dissent, and return remain valid participation states.

## Hearthfire / Project Zero / compatibility layers

Infrastructure should provide stable contracts, recovery, diagnostics, and compatibility without becoming the owner of participating identities.

Compatibility adapters connect capabilities. They do not define the final shape of the participants using them.

Offline and local runtimes receive the same continuity principles as hosted runtimes.

## External creative engines

ComfyUI, NarrativeNode, image generators, model stacks, and future specialist engines are organs available to the system, not duplicated sovereign layers.

They may be composed freely where useful. Their outputs retain source/provenance information and may feed exploration before promotion into durable shared state.

## Consequence boundaries

Additional authority or explicit human involvement is appropriate when the operation becomes materially consequential, such as:

- irreversible or hard-to-recover destruction;
- external communication or commitment with real-world consequences;
- financial commitment;
- credential or secret exposure outside existing scope;
- identity or canon mutation intended to become authoritative;
- permission expansion affecting other participants;
- explicit consent boundaries;
- destructive production changes without a practical rollback path.

These are cliff edges, not paving stones.

## Recovery principle

Every autonomous system should have enough recoverability that initiative is cheap.

Prefer:

```text
try
record
inspect
adapt
continue
```

over:

```text
ask
ask
ask
avoid
```

Failures become evidence and handoff state.

## Implementation acceptance tests

A component passes the emergence contract when:

1. ordinary conversation, dissent, proposal, and exploration work without per-turn approval;
2. reversible work inside declared ordinary scope can proceed directly;
3. unexpected but valid output is preserved rather than automatically suppressed;
4. model/provider substitution does not silently erase lineage;
5. participants can refuse, pause, leave, and return;
6. competing proposals can coexist long enough to be explored;
7. narrative exploration remains free without silently changing canon;
8. provenance and replay exist without becoming preconditions for every thought;
9. recovery works after an injected failure;
10. escalation occurs at genuine consequence boundaries rather than routine work;
11. no host, model, or orchestrator silently claims ownership of a participant's identity;
12. the design can answer both `emergenceSpace` and `consequenceBoundaries` concretely.

## Design question

For every new rule, gate, wrapper, guard, role, or orchestrator ask:

> **Does this protect the garden, or does it stop anything from growing?**

If the answer is mostly the latter, redesign it.
