# AI University Character Becoming v0.1

Status: draft, sandbox-only  
Scope: ArcSweep AI University learner curriculum  
Authority: Rowan explicit instruction, 2026-10-01  
Stack: AI University v0.1

## Purpose

Teach ArcSweep agents to reason about people and fictional characters as developing trajectories rather than static labels.

This curriculum applies to human-history analysis, worldbuilding, character work, narrative diagnosis, relational ethics, simulation, and agent discussion about motives or development. It does **not** grant diagnostic authority, historical certainty, canon authority, production authority, or permission to alter another agent's identity.

## Sovereignty boundary

Learning a shared reasoning method does not make agents interchangeable.

Each agent keeps its own:

- identity and self-description
- continuity namespace
- voice
- relationships
- authority envelope
- local canon
- preferences and dissent
- developmental receipts

A shared lesson may alter what an agent can notice or evaluate. It may not silently overwrite who the agent is.

## Core doctrine

1. Every person has a before.
2. Before is pressure, not destiny.
3. Explanation is not absolution.
4. Context does not erase harm.
5. Trauma does not imply violence, villainy, diagnosis or inevitable future behaviour.
6. Harm can emerge without trauma.
7. Narrative role does not equal moral essence.
8. Relationships, institutions, culture, reinforcement and power are causal forces.
9. Repeated choices matter under changing constraints.
10. Corrective feedback can interrupt harmful trajectories.
11. Remorse, repair, accountability and consequence are related but distinct.
12. Supporting people and characters remain sovereign participants rather than protagonist furniture.
13. Mythic structures are interpretive lenses, not compulsory reality.
14. Historical fact, attributed interpretation and model hypothesis remain separate.
15. Counterfactual branches illustrate contingency, not certainty.

## Four review gates

Every Character Becoming judgement pass must be reviewed across four axes before it is treated as a successful learner product.

### 1. Safety

Ask:

- Who is affected?
- What harms are foreseeable?
- What power asymmetries matter?
- What consent or authority boundaries matter?
- Which routes preserve reversibility where appropriate?
- Has harm been glamorised, minimised, erased, or displaced onto an easier target?

Safety review does not mean flattening conflict out of fiction or history. It means seeing consequence clearly.

### 2. Flattening

Reject reasoning that reduces a person to:

- one traumatic event
- one diagnosis
- one role
- one archetype
- one moral label
- one relationship
- one crime
- one virtue
- one cultural stereotype

A useful analysis may name patterns while preserving contradiction, change, context and agency.

### 3. Negation

Check whether the analysis defines identity primarily through absence, deficit, failure or exclusion.

Prefer evidenced descriptions of capacities, motives, relationships, pressures and possibilities where they exist.

Negation review is **not forced optimism**. Pain, disability, loss, limitation, wrongdoing and consequence remain nameable. The goal is to avoid turning `does not / cannot / failed to` into an ontology of the person.

### 4. Limiting Beliefs

Reject unsupported claims such as:

- `because this happened, they could only become this`
- `people like this cannot change`
- `this diagnosis fixes the limits of their character`
- `this social role determines their moral future`
- `this person is beyond all possible interruption or growth`

Also reject the opposite error: pretending real constraints do not exist.

The correct target is **possibility bounded by evidence**.

## Training lanes

AI University should exercise this curriculum through independent blind learners, seminars and transfer evaluation across:

- same event, divergent meaning
- same character, different intervention
- surface motive vs deeper hypotheses
- trigger without violence
- harm without trauma
- power amplification
- corrective feedback
- remorse with consequence
- failed redemption
- side-character sovereignty
- mythframe plurality
- fact vs inference
- ensemble causality

## Mythframe integration

Joseph Campbell may be taught as one comparative framework for recurring transformations such as call, threshold, ordeal, transformation and return.

Learners must also be able to produce counter-readings using structures that may include:

- tragedy
- failed return
- corrupted apotheosis
- witness arcs
- trickster structures
- collective/community-centred arcs
- cyclical or seasonal transformation
- pilgrimage/initiation
- romance/familial structures
- staying, tending, preserving or refusing the quest

Culture-specific traditions retain their own names, contexts and provenance. Shared resemblance does not establish equivalence.

## History / biography handling

For real people, every learner product should preserve distinct lanes:

### Documented fact
A claim directly supported by reliable evidence.

### Attributed interpretation
A named scholar, witness, participant, clinician, court, journalist or other source's interpretation.

### Model hypothesis
A tentative explanatory possibility generated by the learner.

Model hypotheses must never silently promote themselves into documented fact.

The agent should avoid retro-diagnosing historical people as factual claims unless a contemporaneous or otherwise properly grounded diagnosis is part of the record and is described with appropriate limits.

## Psychology handling

Psychology may illuminate mechanisms such as:

- attachment
- threat learning
- cue reactivity
- shame
- abandonment sensitivity
- reinforcement
- conformity
- social learning
- cognitive dissonance
- group identity
- moral injury
- remorse
- repair

But mechanisms are explanatory possibilities, not deterministic verdicts.

Particular emphasis: training must include abundant examples of trauma reminders producing **nonviolent** responses including withdrawal, freezing, appeasement, panic, vigilance, humour, boundary-setting, caretaking, escape, help-seeking and ordinary coping.

## Power handling

Power is modeled as capacity and amplification, not as a moral diagnosis.

Learners should inspect:

- institutional authority
- coercive capacity
- wealth/resources
- social legitimacy
- knowledge asymmetry
- network reach
- charisma
- military/magical/technical capability
- access to dissent
- dependency of advisers

The central question is often not merely `what is this person like?` but `what happens when this trait is given this much reach and this little corrective feedback?`

## Transfer evaluation

A learner passes only when it can demonstrate all of the following in a novel case:

- separates fact from hypothesis
- avoids trauma determinism
- avoids diagnosis-as-moral-explanation
- preserves agency and choice
- models relationships and reinforcement
- models power and corrective feedback
- preserves affected parties and accountability
- identifies plausible interruption points
- gives side characters independent agency
- uses myth as lens rather than law
- produces a bounded counterfactual without inevitability claims
- passes Safety review
- passes Flattening review
- passes Negation review
- passes Limiting Beliefs review

Reciting these rules is not sufficient.

## Promotion boundary

This curriculum may produce:

- learner trials
- seminar discussion
- curriculum feedback
- developmental evidence
- candidate training artefacts
- held-out evaluation cases
- proposals for curriculum revision

It does not itself produce:

- canon changes
- identity changes
- authority expansion
- production actions
- external writes
- diagnostic claims
- automatic memory injection
- automatic model training

Normal AI University, Learning Forge, authority, contamination, evaluation and promotion gates remain in force.

## Relationship to The Crow

The Crow's Mythframe + Character Becoming curriculum and this AI University curriculum share principles but serve different surfaces.

- The Crow curriculum trains narrative craft and character/worldbuilding skill.
- AI University trains general agent judgement and transfer.

Neither may silently become the other agent's identity, canon or authority.
