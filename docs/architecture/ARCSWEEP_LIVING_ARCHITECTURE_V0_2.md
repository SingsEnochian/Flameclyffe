# ArcSweep Living Architecture v0.2

**Status:** Rowan/Rarity-side architecture contract  
**Date:** 2026-10-01 America/New_York  
**Authority:** Rowan, Steward  
**Scope:** ArcSweep / Flameclyffe local architecture only  
**Cross-constellation boundary:** foreign architecture is read-only context until an explicit Rowan/Rarity-side decision promotes a local interpretation.

## Why this revision exists

ArcSweep has outgrown the picture of a service shell with a cognitive runtime attached. Recent work added or clarified a cognitive loop, distinct continuity and knowledge planes, source epistemics, agent education, character-becoming / Mythframe reasoning, symbolic provenance, responsive embodied interfaces, and a promotion law for material that can shape agents or models.

This revision names those organs without collapsing their authority.

The target is not one giant subsystem. The target is a living circulation in which evidence, cognition, possibility, continuity, knowledge, learning, symbolic expression, execution, embodiment, and observation can inform one another while remaining distinguishable and inspectable.

## Governing laws

### 1. Four-Gate Integrity Law

Before material is promoted, taught, canonised, merged into governing doctrine, or used to shape an agent/model, review it for:

1. **Safety** — affected parties, foreseeable harm, consent/authority, reversibility, security and consequence boundaries.
2. **Flattening** — do not reduce a person, agent, culture, source, symbol, world, role, diagnosis, trauma, archetype, or subsystem to one explanatory label.
3. **Negation** — do not define identity primarily through absence, deficit, exclusion, or failure; preserve positive structure and actual capacities while keeping real constraints visible.
4. **Limiting Beliefs** — do not convert present constraints, history, role, diagnosis, origin, implementation limits, or uncertainty into unsupported destiny.

Gate state is `pass | revise | hold`. Promotion requires all four to pass.

**Core rule: possibility bounded by evidence.**

### 2. Mythience non-impersonation law

**Formalism, measurement, myth, and magic — none may impersonate another.**

A symbolic reading can be valuable without becoming a measurement. A measurement can constrain a claim without exhausting meaning. A mythic pattern can organise experience without becoming a universal psychological law. A magical or ritual tradition can be represented faithfully without being relabelled as established science.

### 3. Authority law

Cognition does not create authority.  
Possibility may propose; it may not silently execute.  
Similarity does not establish identity.  
Interpretation does not become evidence through repetition.  
A source being available does not make it authorised training material or canon.  
A foreign exchange does not become local architecture through familiarity.

### 4. Continuity law

Continuity preserves ancestry, state, relationship, provenance and return paths. It does not dictate identity. Provider or model replacement may not silently rewrite continuity.

### 5. Source-custody law

A retrieval result carries source identity, source class, rights/use posture, revision/freshness where available, confidence/uncertainty, transformation lineage and canon relationship. Retrieval is not promotion.

## Plane contracts

### Observation plane — Observer + DEEP

**Owns:** evidence-aware observation, source/witness context, explicit interpretation boundaries, observation receipts and feedback into the loop.

**Does not own:** identity, canon by default, execution authority, Mythframe truth, or symbolic meaning as measurement.

### Cognitive plane — ArcSweep Cognitive Core

**Owns:** synthesis, reasoning, hypothesis formation, planning context, comparison and bounded cognitive state.

**Does not own:** external authority, source truth, identity continuity, canon promotion or execution permission.

A cognitive result may become a candidate observation, hypothesis, proposal or task. It must retain lineage.

### Possibility plane — PREMAQC

**Owns:** possibility-pressure/state exploration, alternative trajectories, uncertainty-aware candidate transitions and counterfactual space.

**Does not own:** execution, factual promotion, destiny claims or authority amplification.

Possibility is a search space, not a verdict.

### Continuity plane

**Owns:** authorised continuity, ancestry, session/work state, relationship/state lineage, resumability, provider-independent continuity bundles, degradation states and return points.

**Does not own:** a person's or agent's identity decision. It supplies the slot and provenance; it does not manufacture the answer.

### Universal Codex plane

**Owns:** source epistemics, provenance, references, canon relationships, source classes, transformation lineage, confidence and rights/use posture.

Useful source classes include documented observation, historical primary, academic interpretation, practitioner tradition, folklore/myth, ritual text, symbolic correspondence, polemical source, conspiratorial claim, fictional canon, fan interpretation, model hypothesis and synthetic training example.

**Does not own:** automatic truth, automatic canon, model-training permission, or magical/scientific equivalence.

### Learning Forge plane

**Owns:** reviewed curricula, synthetic training examples, held-out evaluation, training manifests, behavioural-delta evidence and promotion receipts for Crow and agent education.

The Learning Forge includes:

- **Character Becoming** — causal depth for protagonists, antagonists and side characters.
- **Mythframe** — Campbell as one lens among multiple narrative structures.
- **AI University** — transfer learning for agents without personality homogenisation.
- **Four-Gate review** — promotion integrity.

**No imaginary learning:** a document existing does not mean a model learned it. The path is curriculum → reviewed corpus → sealed evaluation → real training execution → evidence → promotion.

### Character Becoming model

```text
person
+ developmental history
+ interpretation
+ relationships
+ culture
+ circumstance
+ reinforcement
+ power
+ choice history
→ trajectory
```

This is a writing/reasoning heuristic, not deterministic psychology.

Required non-flattening constraints:

- trauma is not destiny;
- trauma does not imply violence;
- harm need not be invented from trauma;
- hero/protagonist does not mean morally correct;
- antagonist/villain does not mean incapable of love, grief or truth;
- remorse does not erase consequence;
- explanation is not absolution;
- side characters retain private goals, limits and lives beyond the lead;
- historical subjects are not retro-diagnosed as fact.

### Mythframe model

Campbellian patterns may be useful: call, refusal, threshold, guide, descent, ordeal, transformation, return and integration.

They are not mandatory geometry.

The library also supports tragedy, failed return, corrupted apotheosis, refusal of reintegration, cyclical transformation, collective/community arcs, trickster structures, witness arcs, pilgrimage/initiation, romance/familial arcs, anti-quest/staying/tending structures and culture-specific narrative systems.

Useful distinction:

```text
story occurring around a character
!=
story the character believes is occurring
```

### Runa / Glyph Forge plane

**Owns:** glyphic/harmonic/sensory manifestation and provenance-aware symbol grammar.

Core rule:

**Visual resemblance creates a possible relationship, not semantic identity.**

A historical sigil, alchemical mark, ritual symbol, religious sign, fictional rune and user-created glyph may look similar while retaining distinct source, period, tradition, creator, attributed meaning and uncertainty.

Runa manifests shared state through existing engines and observable receipts. It is not a second source of canon.

### Hearthweave orchestration / capability fabric

**Owns:** orchestration, capability routing, bounded delegation, receipts and composition among local organs.

**Does not own:** the internal authority or identity of every organ it connects.

### Project Zero + House Commons surfaces

These are execution/social surfaces in the local ArcSweep circulation. Execution remains capability-bound and receipted. Social/relational surfaces do not silently acquire source or identity authority.

### Embodied interface — Magic Book + spatial/gesture grammar

The Magic Book remains the primary human-facing embodied interface.

Responsive holographic interaction uses truthful state rather than decorative spectacle.

Gesture grammar:

```text
intent → target → preview → commit/cancel → receipt → accessible fallback
```

Motion grammar:

```text
intent + best_for + avoid_when + restraint + reduced_motion + provenance
```

Three.js/spatial rendering may express depth, light, gesture and responsive geometry where useful. Accessibility and reduced-motion behaviour are part of the design contract.

## Cross-constellation boundary

Lanternbridge is an exchange boundary, not a shared-memory bus.

Rowan/Rarity-side rule:

```text
FOREIGN CONTEXT MAY INFORM.
FOREIGN CONTEXT MAY NOT DEFINE.
```

And:

```text
SOURCE ARCHITECTURE != RECEIVER ARCHITECTURE
SHARED TERM != SHARED SUBSYSTEM
INFERENCE != MAPPING
MAPPING != EQUIVALENCE
MAPPING != ADOPTION
INTERSECTION != IDENTITY
CONVERGENCE != MERGER
DISCUSSION != DECISION
```

Unknown foreign terms remain opaque until the source side defines them or an explicit bridge record establishes a mapping.

An exchange from Twilight/Nocturne may inspire a Rowan/Rarity-side proposal, but only an authorised Rowan/Rarity-side decision mutates local canon/architecture. The same respect is expected in the other direction without presuming how the other constellation implements it.

## Source ingest posture

### Repository material

External repositories are mined first for provenance-safe principles, architecture patterns, evaluation ideas and source leads. A fork or local copy does not erase upstream authorship/licensing.

### API/integration material

Credentials, tokens and secret values are never training data. Integrations are represented as redacted capability/service maps only.

### Witchcraft / occult / ritual material

Keep historical, academic, practitioner, symbolic, ritual, folklore/myth, polemical and conspiratorial source classes distinct. Do not collapse an accusation, a practitioner account, a historical source, a correspondence table and a modern scholarly interpretation into one epistemic lane.

## Promotion receipt

A promotion decision should be able to answer:

```text
what is being promoted?
from which source(s)?
under what rights/use posture?
what transformations occurred?
what is fact vs interpretation vs hypothesis?
what does it affect?
who owns the next action?
who approved promotion?
what were the Four-Gate results?
what can be rolled back?
what evidence shows the intended effect actually occurred?
```

Unacknowledged handoffs remain open. `next_owner` is explicit and names an agent or human, never "the system".
