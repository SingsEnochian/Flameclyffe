## Terra Prime

Terra Prime is the 1:1 current-reality reference world. Its clock advances with ordinary observed time: one Terra Prime second per one observed second. The endpoint may add UTC, Julian date, local civil time, solar/lunar geometry and source-native timestamps as temporal anchors.

Terra Prime is fed continuously through Observer rather than through a one-time canon scrape.

Initial source families:

- time;
- astronomy;
- space weather;
- Earth systems;
- geophysics;
- science and research;
- selected human-world events;
- project-origin observations from Observer and Arcsweep.

## Bridge braid

```text
External sources
```

Neither endpoint is treated as a decorative selector. Each shore exposes enough state to answer a crossing.

## Endpoint contract

Each shore exposes:

- world identity;
- temporal relation;
- Observer freshness;
- PREMAQC;
- Spiral phase, direction and confidence;
- world semantic profile;
- canon/context register;
- glyph interpretation;
- Runa interpretation;
- model/storywork context;
- receipts.

A shore is `lit` when its required current state is available to the bridge. A dark lantern reports the missing organ explicitly.

## Crossing law

A crossing translates through relation rather than copying source values directly.

Working packet:

```text
B_A→B = {
  source_state,
  desired_state,
  source_receipts,
  departure_time,
  source_world,
  destination_world,
  invariants
}
```

The destination answers with a translated state, changed relationships, destination timestamp, glyph, Runa plan, story/character implications, advisory suggestions and crossing receipt.

## Candidate bridge invariants

World-local expression may transform: tone, glyph geometry, sound palette, narrative diction, environmental response, character expression and temporal rhythm.

`UNTRANSLATABLE` names a discovered world-local boundary and is receipted as an outcome.

## Spiral Holonomy

For a closed world-crossing circuit, Arcsweep records the engine-defined relational diagnostic

```text
H_loop = d(S_original, B_loop(S_original))
```

This imports the mathematical idea of holonomy into the engine to measure what the completed crossing circuit changed. A second comparison against the later observed Terra Prime state separates journey transformation from ordinary 1:1 Terra Prime evolution.
