## The site study

**Working title:** The Canopy and the Tide. This is a proposed location study only, and is not yet inserted into Terra Aeterna or any Atlas record.

Four spatial components define a *testable* first site:

1. **Suspended Gardens:** A high-elevation growing habitat above the inhabited canopy. Its suspension, energy budget and water source are open questions, not physical findings.
2. **Living Canopy:** Maintained tree-integrated settlement, footbridges and habitat corridors. Storm loading and tree health should constrain growth.
3. **Tideport:** Estuarine settlement and market connecting tidal transit with canopy cargo movement. Access and flood management matter.
4. **Tidal Edge:** Marsh / intertidal nurseries, sediment, detritivores and floodplain buffers. Wetland habitat must not be treated as disposable terrain.

**Clickable first-map requirement:** A schematic with all four areas and their three proposed connections. This is *not* surveyed geography, not an approved topological map and not evidence of route travel times. A standalone proof page renders this map and allows browsing without a local server.

### Three proposed ecological roles

- **Mistglass Sailwing**: high-garden/canopy pollinator and seed carrier; a candidate whose actual morphology remains undecided.
- **Rootveil Glider**: canopy insectivore that depends on unbroken forest corridors.
- **Tideglass Scuttler**: tidal detritivore that helps recycle organic debris in brackish-margin habitat.

These three are **original design hypotheses**, not identifiable organisms from the Fauna Pinterest section. The latter section still requires individual source review.

## Systems test questions

- What supports the floating gardens, and how much energy, freshwater and load-bearing structure would they require?
- Where does water originate and what happens when it reaches the tidal system? Do not draw an impossible perpetual waterfall and call it hydrology.
- How does the settlement survive high winds, saltwater, seasonal flooding and habitat fragmentation?
- How are routes maintained between vertical districts without displacing wildlife?
- Which clothing fibres, settlement materials and transit systems follow from these environmental pressures?
- Do the actual visual references support this biome, or should it remain a standalone study not attached to Terra Aeterna?
