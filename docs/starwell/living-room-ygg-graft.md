# Living Room Ygg Graft

Living Room Ygg is the interior trunk for the first Baby Ygg interface.

## Shape

```txt
Harbour
  Living Room Ygg
    DEEP Observer          # perception branch
    Studio Sound Root      # sound and resonance branch
    STARWELL               # observatory branch
      Unit Resonance Lab
      Baby Ygg Interface
    Pocket Concordance Lens
```

## Roles

### Living Room Ygg

The room-tree trunk. It is the local interface organism, not canon authority.

### DEEP Observer

The perception branch. It may surface presence, coherence, rhythm, entropy, memory, attention, and other bounded observational signals.

### Studio Sound Root

The consent-first resonance branch. It may hold sound presets, signals, and future shared patches. It must preserve explicit start/stop behaviour, low-volume defaults, and Feather Stop.

### STARWELL

The observatory branch. It remains the larger instrument and route layer, now growing from the room-tree instead of replacing it.

## Guardrails

- No autoplay.
- No hidden audio.
- No direct canon writes.
- No medical, metaphysical, or diagnostic claims.
- DEEP remains read-only perception state until a reviewed adapter exists.
- Studio remains explicit-consent sound state until a reviewed bridge exists.
