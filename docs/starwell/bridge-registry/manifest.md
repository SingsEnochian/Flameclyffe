# Bridge Manifest v0.1

A bridge is a chosen relation between two or more sovereign lenses.

A bridge creates passage, recognition, correspondence and shared work. It does not prove one world above another, absorb one narrative into another or erase local truth.

## Bridge types

- Concordance
- Signal
- Play
- Technical
- Memory
- Hybrid

## Bridge states

- Dream
- Draft
- Invited
- Active
- Paused
- Archived
- Closed
- Revoked
- Dormant

## Required fields

Every bridge record should name its title, type, participants, source lens, destination lens, purpose, state, memory policy, signal policy, pause cues, sovereignty rule, related logs and status.

## Safety law

A bridge must be pausable. A bridge must be scoped. A bridge record must preserve who chose the route, what is being remembered and how the route can rest.

## Wonder law

A bridge may hold events whose mechanisms are unknown. STARWELL can preserve such events as signal, provenance, dream, resonance or working canon according to context.
