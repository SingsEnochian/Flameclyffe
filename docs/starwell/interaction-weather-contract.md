# Interaction Weather Contract v0.1

Status: draft contract for Portal Kernel v0.1

Interaction Weather turns input into gentle world response. Typing, touch, pointer drift, scrolling, focus, idle state, device motion, gaze, sound, and haptics may influence the living interface only when allowed by consent, accessibility, and room policy.

## Core vow

Reaction is not surveillance. The system may respond to rhythm, pause, activity, and presence without pretending to know the user's soul from behaviour traces.

## Input lanes

- `typing`: cadence, pause, drafting, revision, save events.
- `touch`: tap, long press, drag, pinch, pressure where supported.
- `pointer`: hover, drift, drag, focus target.
- `scroll`: traversal, reading pace, arrival and departure.
- `motion`: tilt or device movement only after permission.
- `gaze`: future accessibility lane, explicit permission only.
- `sound`: consented input/output state only.
- `haptics`: consented output state only.

## Embodiment state

Inputs are softened into presence, creation, revision, exploration, stillness, and traversal. These values may drive branch growth, firefly density, pulse speed, root glow, canopy calm, or label clarity.

## Accessibility clamps

Reduced motion, high contrast, reduced transparency, sensory quiet, mute, plain pass, and keyboard navigation override expressive response. Still magic is preferred over sad beige fallback.

## Narrative boundary

Writing activity may wake story branches, buds, scars, flowers, or fruit. It may not canonize changes, infer private meaning, or rewrite narrative direction without approval.
