Harmonic Canon

score bible seed

The Chapel begins the recurring musical language for Flameclyffe and Terra Aeterna.

  • Gabriel: warm baritone, open fifths into major sixths, adaptive resonance.
  • Sariel: first soprano, overtone carrier, suspended ascents, crystalline tuning.
  • Throne: subharmonic drone, cathedral bass, architecture remembering.
  • Veil: suspended clusters resolving into breath and light.
  • Transformata: awakening motif, repetition that becomes recognition.

Terra Aeterna Integration

living world

In Terra Aeterna, the songs are discovered Calls. The Veil Shall Lift opens almost-doors. The Transformata becomes the first inner-awakening Call added to the Living Liturgy.
