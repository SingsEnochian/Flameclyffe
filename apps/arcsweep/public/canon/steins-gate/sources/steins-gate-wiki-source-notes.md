## Extracted canon map
- Steins;Gate is the second Science Adventure entry; the original visual novel released in Japan in 2009 and is primarily set in Akihabara in 2010.
- A world line is a complete self-consistent history. The setting presents many possible world lines but only one physically realised active world line at a time; inactive lines remain possibilities.
- A divergence value identifies a world line. A shift reconstructs history to the newly active line, so ordinary memories normally update with it.
- Attractor fields are bundles of world lines that converge on characteristic outcomes even when local details differ.
- Alpha world lines converge toward SERN domination; Beta world lines converge toward World War III. The Steins Gate world line, divergence 1.048596%, avoids the principal convergence outcomes of both.
- World Line Convergence means local interventions may change circumstances without changing the convergent result. Escaping a convergence requires a change large enough to enter a different attractor field.
- Reading Steiner is Okabe's name for his anomalous preservation of memories across world-line shifts. Other people can retain weaker echoes as dreams or déjà vu.
- The Divergence Meter is a future Okabe device used with Reading Steiner to identify world-line/attractor-field changes.
- D-Mails are short messages sent to the past through the PhoneWave and can activate another world line when their effects contradict prior history.
- The Time Leap Machine transmits a person's memory data to their own earlier brain. The ordinary Alpha-era safe interval is 48 hours; later work demonstrates longer jumps under different implementations.
- Operation Skuld succeeds by preserving what Okabe's past self must observe while changing the causal conditions that would otherwise lead to the Alpha/Beta catastrophes.
- The Future Gadget Laboratory is both a maker-space and social group. Its members' relationships are causally central to the series rather than incidental framing.
- Amadeus is an AI architecture built from digitised human memory/personality data. Amadeus Kurisu becomes especially important in Steins;Gate 0 and in attempts to recover Kurisu's time-travel research.
