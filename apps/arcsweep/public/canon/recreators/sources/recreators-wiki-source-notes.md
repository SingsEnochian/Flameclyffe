## Extracted canon map
- Re:CREATORS is a 22-episode 2017 anime created/written by Rei Hiroe with series composition/direction by Ei Aoki and animation by TROYCA.
- The setting distinguishes the Real World, creators in that world, and fictional Storyworlds whose characters can become "Created" in the Real World.
- The wiki's Created page describes Real-World arrival as freeing Created characters from the plot-bound emotional/narrative constraints of their home stories.
- The character category currently enumerates 25 character pages; the Creations category enumerates 13 creation-related entries; the Creators category enumerates 8 creator entries.
- Altair is also called the Military Uniform Princess. She is Setsuna/Yuna Shimazaki's secondary creation, based on Shirotsumekusa from the in-universe game Eternal Wars Megalosphere and popularised through the in-universe video Altair: World Étude.
- Altair is described as an independent existence without a story, aware of creators and the distinction between fictional worlds and the Real World.
- Altair brings Created characters into the Real World and seeks a Great Destruction after Setsuna's death.
- Holopsicon is Altair's evolving ability set. The wiki attributes world travel/transport, deletion or erasure effects, telekinesis, flight, defensive sword formations, and additional powers gained through derivative portrayals to it.
- Audience acceptance is narratively important to whether additions, revisions, or creations persist. Sirius's confrontation with Altair is a key demonstration of this mechanism.
- Setsuna/Yuna Shimazaki is Altair's creator and Sōta Mizushino's former friend. The late-series resolution places Setsuna and Altair together in a newly created world, with reciprocal authorship imagery.
