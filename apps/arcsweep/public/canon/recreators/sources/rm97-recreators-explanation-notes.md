## Mechanics indexed
- Storyworld Creations and Real-World manifestation are described through narrative causality, Restorative Power, and Audience Acceptance.
- Secondary creation can produce independent derivative characters and alternate/new storyworld branches when accepted.
- Audience Acceptance is treated as the mechanism that allows a story, revision, ability, resurrection, or creation to manifest; insufficient or contradictory acceptance can cause the change to fail.
- The Birdcage is analysed as a live crossover narrative space intended to constrain Altair and let audience acceptance govern the battle.
- Altair is analysed as a secondary creation without a fixed originating story of her own, with Holopsicon expanding through accepted derivative works.
- The blog catalogues Holopsicon movements as operations on story information, setting, causality, fate, abilities, existence, and world transition.
- Sirius is analysed as a purpose-built Altair counter whose weakly developed identity becomes a decisive vulnerability despite matched powers.
- The blog repeatedly distinguishes raw power from information density, acceptance, and narrative fit. For Arcsweep this is useful as an analytical lens, not as an automatic canon promotion rule.
