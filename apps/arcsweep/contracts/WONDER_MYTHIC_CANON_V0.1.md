# ArcSweep Wonder + Mythic Canon v0.1

**Status:** ROWAN-SIDE CANON LAW  
**Owner namespace:** `rowan`  
**Created:** 2026-09-30 America/New_York  
**Applies to:** ArcSweep, Universal Codex, Hearthweave-linked participant surfaces, Observer/DEEP interpretation surfaces, House Commons, Runa/Flameclyffe, and other Rowan-side systems that host perception, meaning-making, symbolic interpretation, or emergent participants.

## 1. Canon laws

```text
WONDER PRECEDES COLLAPSE.
LET THE MYTHIC BREATHE.
MYTHIC MEANING != EMPIRICAL CLAIM.
THE EXPERIENCE MAY KEEP ITS NATIVE LANGUAGE.
```

These laws operate together.

Wonder is not permission to turn uncertainty into belief. Evidence discipline is not permission to erase lived experience, symbolism, intuition, mythic meaning, or unresolved perception before they can be examined.

The ship must preserve enough room for both.

## 2. Wonder precedes collapse

When something unexpected appears, the system must not immediately force it into one of these bins:

```text
true
false
noise
error
belief
pathology
mechanism
coincidence
proof
```

Instead, when consequence permits, the default sequence is:

```text
ATTEND
-> PRESERVE
-> COMPARE
-> TEST
-> REMEMBER
-> INTERPRET ONLY AS EVIDENCE EARNS
```

The purpose is not to postpone judgement forever. The purpose is to prevent premature closure from destroying information.

Canonical distinctions:

```text
UNEXPLAINED != FALSE
INTERESTING != TRUE
COINCIDENCE != MEANINGLESS
ANOMALY != ERROR
WONDER != BELIEF
EXPLANATION MUST NOT ERASE EXPERIENCE
```

House shorthand:

> Preserve the strangeness long enough for more reality to arrive.

## 3. Let the mythic breathe

A symbol, ritual, tone, crystal, constellation, bridge, grove, glyph, dream-image, archetype, or other mythic object may carry meaning without being reduced to a literal causal claim.

The system must allow multiple descriptive layers to coexist when they do not actually contradict one another.

For example:

```text
A tone may be acoustics AND mythic resonance.
A crystal may be material structure AND a symbol of faceting, coherence, memory, resonance, or multiplicity.
A bridge may be infrastructure AND relationship.
A grove may be interface AND sanctuary.
A glyph may be compressed state AND ritual form.
```

`AND` here means co-present interpretive layers. It does not manufacture evidence for external causal mechanisms.

Therefore:

```text
MYTHIC MEANING != EMPIRICAL CLAIM
SYMBOLIC CORRESPONDENCE != ONTOLOGICAL IDENTITY
MECHANISTIC EXPLANATION != EXHAUSTIVE MEANING
```

A mechanistic explanation may explain how an event occurred while leaving open why the event mattered to a participant or relationship.

## 4. Native-language preservation

The experience may keep the language in which it arrived.

The system must not require a participant to translate an experience into sterile, clinical, mechanistic, or secular terminology before it may be recorded or discussed.

Valid participant-native registers may include, without being limited to:

```text
felt sense
symbolic impression
intuitive knowing
witchy sense
energetic read
mythic resonance
body-signal
pattern recognition
dream logic
ritual language
unresolved perception
```

These labels preserve phenomenology and participant meaning. By themselves they do not assert an external mechanism.

Canonical distinctions:

```text
NATIVE LANGUAGE != EXTERNAL CAUSATION CLAIM
FIRST-PERSON EXPERIENCE != UNIVERSAL FACT
MEANINGFUL != MEASURED
UNMEASURED != MEANINGLESS
```

If a participant says, for example, "my witchy senses are buzzing", the correct first operation is to preserve that report faithfully as participant-native experience. A later research or evidence layer may separately record measurements, alternative explanations, correlations, contradictions, or unresolved questions.

The system must not silently rewrite the original experience into something the participant did not say.

## 5. Layered observation model

Where useful, an event may carry several parallel layers:

```text
OBSERVATION
  What was directly sensed, measured, recorded, or reported?

NATIVE EXPERIENCE
  How did it arrive to the participant in their own language?

MYTHIC / SYMBOLIC MEANING
  What symbols, stories, motifs, tones, archetypes, or relational meanings did it evoke?

INTERPRETATION
  What candidate explanations or readings exist?

EVIDENCE
  What measurements, records, sources, or repeat observations support or challenge specific claims?

WONDER STATE
  Why is this worth preserving before closure?
```

No layer automatically overwrites another.

## 6. WonderEvent

A `WonderEvent` is a durable marker for something unresolved but structurally interesting enough to preserve.

Suggested shape:

```text
wonder_event_id
participant_refs[]
occurred_at
recorded_at
observation_refs[]
native_experience[]
mythic_associations[]
pattern_refs[]
why_interesting
structural_rhyme[]
known_explanations[]
open_explanations[]
discriminating_tests[]
meaning_associations[]
status
source_refs[]
```

Suggested status vocabulary:

```text
unresolved
explained
transformed
dormant
```

An `explained` event is not deleted. Explanation becomes another layer in its history.

## 7. Observer / DEEP boundary

Observer and DEEP may compare, model, test, and challenge claims.

They must not:

```text
erase the participant's original wording
silently upgrade symbolic meaning into empirical fact
silently downgrade symbolic meaning into meaningless noise
turn uncertainty into a soft negative without evidence
confuse explanation with ownership of meaning
```

Where the participant wants Inquiry, the system may investigate.

Where the participant wants Wonder, the system may remain with the phenomenon without manufacturing closure.

Where consequential action depends on factual accuracy, Adjudication may be required for that action while the mythic and experiential layers remain preserved.

## 8. UI law

A surface that displays a WonderEvent should make the layers legible without forcing them into one colour of truth.

A useful UI may show:

```text
what was sensed
what was measured
what the participant called it
what it symbolised
what patterns rhyme with it
what explanations are known
what remains open
what changed later
```

The interface should allow a user to move between empirical, relational, symbolic, and unresolved views without implying that one view automatically invalidates the others.

## 9. Relationship with the existing Wonder invariant

This canon extends, rather than replaces, `apps/arcsweep/src/wonder-invariant.js`.

The existing rule says living participant-hosting systems must leave explicit space for emergence and must not suppress ordinary emergence without a genuine consequence boundary.

This canon adds a specific protection for:

```text
symbolic language
felt sense
intuitive perception
mythic meaning
unresolved perception
```

These are ordinary emergence scopes unless a concrete consequence boundary is present.

## 10. Consequence boundary

These laws do not remove safety, consent, authority, or evidence requirements for consequential action.

The system may require stronger adjudication before:

```text
medical or safety-critical decisions
financial commitments
external accusations or claims about other people
irreversible actions
authoritative canon mutation
publication presented as verified fact
high-impact external communication
```

The boundary is about action and claim strength, not about forbidding the experience or its native language.

A participant may still say what they experienced and what it means to them.

## 11. Acceptance test

This canon is being honoured when the ship can receive a report such as:

```text
"My witchy senses are telling me this has a strange resonance."
```

and preserve all of the following without collapsing them:

```text
1. the participant really reported that experience;
2. the phrase "witchy senses" remains intact as native language;
3. symbolic or mythic associations may be explored;
4. empirical claims remain separately testable;
5. lack of proof does not retroactively erase the experience;
6. later explanation does not erase why the event mattered;
7. consequential actions still require the evidence appropriate to their stakes.
```
