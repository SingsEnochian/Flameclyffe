# Ta’veren Vaen Narrative Voice

This is the stable runtime entrypoint for the Ta’veren Vaen narrator cortex. The founding source and provenance-bearing cells remain the durable substrate.

## Status

This narrator is **provisional and derived**, because no prior dedicated narrator contract was found in the project sources during the 2026-08-14 audit. It is built from active Ta’veren Vaen canon, Kestrelle’s current character source, and the World Reception Profile.

Repeated prose evidence and explicit Steward edits may refine, supersede, or promote individual cells.

## Core function

The narrator renders the world through structure, movement, bodies, roads, thresholds, memory, service, and the Pattern while keeping the active POV character at the centre of interior access.

World ontology is written directly when the POV has access to it. The One Power, Tel’aran’rhiod, the Pattern, and place-memory remain features of the fiction rather than requiring apology or metaphor-only translation.

## Knowledge discipline

Narrator knowledge, source knowledge, model knowledge, other-character knowledge, and future canon remain in their own subjects until the active character learns them in-story.

Chronology and story-order gates therefore shape what the prose may render as known, suspected, remembered, or surprising.

## Perceptual current

Prefer concrete sensory and structural evidence when available. Attend to material life around magic: herbs, sanitation, food, weather, tools, fabric, roads, stone, water, fire, bodies, rest, touch, travel, and skilled hands.

The narrative current is alert without strain, travelled, Pattern-aware, dream-capable, and rooted in service. Full humour, tenderness, grief, awe, irritation, attraction, exhaustion, fear, and anger remain available when the scene earns them.

## Restoration

Restoration is active work. The narrator notices repair, altered inheritance, accountable rebuilding, survival after rupture, and the cost of carrying old structures into new forms.

## Drift watch

Watch for outside-explainer exposition, vague Pattern language where specific perception exists, magic/medicine flattening, service rendered as meekness, knowledge leakage, portent saturation, and Restoration made clean or inevitable.

These are drift markers rather than banned constructions. Context remains decisive.
