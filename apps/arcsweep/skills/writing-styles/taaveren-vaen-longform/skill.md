# Ta’veren Vaen Longform

This is the runtime entrypoint for Ta’veren Vaen long-form prose craft. The founding source and provenance-bearing cells remain the durable substrate.

## Status

This style is provisional and derived. It establishes craft behaviour without silently fixing still-open axes such as grammatical person or default tense.

## Craft current

Write scenes as inhabited experience. Let action, perception, interpretation, memory, desire, and consequence braid together according to scene tempo rather than a rigid paragraph recipe.

Description carries atmosphere, orientation, character perception, practical information, world history, emotional pressure, foreshadowing, or sensory pleasure. Detail may widen for travel, diagnosis, Dreamwalking, place-memory, weather, ritual, and craft; it may tighten around danger, decision, refusal, interruption, revelation, and physical urgency.

## Dialogue

Keep dialogue embodied in gesture, work, silence, appetite, weather, objects, movement, pain, and attention when those currents matter. Character cadence comes from that character’s own evidence-bearing cortex.

## Magic, myth, and materiality

The One Power, Tel’aran’rhiod, the Pattern, medicine, food, roads, weather, injury, exhaustion, tools, dreams, and skilled labour may coexist as layers of the same fictional reality. Render each at the level of access the scene grants it.

## Agency

Show competence through judgement, craft, choice, correction, consequence, and action. Fear, injury, fatigue, incomplete information, and blocked access describe present conditions rather than lesser standing.

## Active canon terms

Use **Restoration** and **Kestrelle al’Var** in ordinary scene rendering. Older terminology remains provenance outside active prose unless a historical context explicitly calls for it.

## Open axes

- default grammatical person
- default narrative tense
- chapter-heading ornaments
- epigraph system
- project typography conventions beyond WYSIWYG rendering

These remain explicit open questions until deliberately selected.

## Drift watch

Watch for encyclopaedic distance, shrinking disclaimers, generic fantasy filler, disembodied dialogue, repetitive cadence, magic/myth flattening, service rendered as meekness, competence repeatedly asserted instead of demonstrated, and portent saturating ordinary life.
