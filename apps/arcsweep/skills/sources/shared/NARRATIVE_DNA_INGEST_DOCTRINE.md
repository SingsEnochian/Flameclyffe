# Narrative DNA Ingest Doctrine v0.1

Status: active shared doctrine  
Scope: ArcSweep / The Crow / AI University Character Becoming / creative research  
Authority: Rowan explicit instruction, 2026-10-01

## Core instruction

**Ingest and learn for everything.**

When a source, script, world pitch, game system, writing guide, roleplay transcript, image board, palette archive, conlang discussion, interface, or external creative tool is studied, do not stop at surface content. Extract the transferable structure beneath it.

The goal is not imitation. The goal is to learn what makes a source function, represent that knowledge explicitly, preserve provenance, and make the lesson reusable without flattening local canon, identity, or voice.

## Multi-layer ingest

Inspect as many of these layers as are relevant:

1. **Surface content** — what is explicitly present.
2. **Purpose** — the job it performs for writer, reader, player, designer, or system.
3. **Interaction grammar** — verbs, choices, affordances, loops, and constraints.
4. **State model** — what exists, what can change, and what persists.
5. **Narrative mechanics** — pressure, reveal, reversal, trial, quest, setup, payoff, callback, obligation, vulnerability, consequence.
6. **Character mechanics** — wants, beliefs, fears, loyalties, capabilities, wounds, identity claims, and transitions.
7. **Relationship mechanics** — trust, dependence, intimacy, resentment, obligation, secrecy, permission, attraction, power, and changed future affordances.
8. **World mechanics** — laws, resources, institutions, geography, ecology, culture, technology, magic, history, and environmental pressure.
9. **Aesthetic mechanics** — colour, material, rhythm, contrast, motion, sound, typography, composition, symbolic recurrence.
10. **Failure signals** — repetition, incoherence, genericity, inaccessible design, contradiction, inert scenes, under-motivated action, over-generation.
11. **Authorial attractors** — recurring patterns across an authorised corpus that may represent themes, fascinations, habits, or unresolved questions.
12. **Transferable principle** — the smallest useful lesson that can survive outside the source.

## Typed story atoms

Do not collapse every structure into one `trope` bucket. Prefer typed atoms:

- pressure

- world_condition

- object

- vulnerability

- setup
- payoff
- callback
- resource_constraint
- environmental_rule

One element may perform several functions simultaneously.

## Recurrence is evidence, not destiny

When a pattern recurs across a creator's authorised corpus, surface it as a **narrative attractor**.

Ask whether the recurrence is intentional, thematic, structural, habitual, or a blind spot. Offer both deepening and subversion experiments. Never turn recurrence into a compulsory generation rule.

`The author often writes X` must never silently become `therefore always generate X`.

## Extract, type, cluster, deduplicate, preserve

Use this pass after broad extraction:

```text
extract
→ type
→ cluster related structures
→ deduplicate literal repetition
→ preserve meaningful distinctions
→ record provenance
```

Similar labels remain distinct when their dramatic functions differ.

Narrative-DNA observations are analysis, not canon and not identity truth.

## Character Becoming integration

For each meaningful event inspect:

```text
state before
pressure / opportunity
choice
cost
relationship delta
knowledge delta
capability delta
belief evidence
identity evidence
state after
future possibilities opened
future possibilities closed
```

Character is trajectory through repeated choices under changing pressure.

## Relationship Becoming integration

Use the reusable grammar:

```text
attention
→ familiarity
→ privileged access
→ dependence
→ recognition
→ cost
→ choice
→ transformation
```

This applies beyond romance to friendship, rivalry, family, mentorship, alliance, antagonism, and found family. Important scenes should produce a relationship delta.

## The Crow learning loop

For a source or authorised corpus, separate:

1. explicit content;
2. observed structural patterns;
3. inference with confidence;
4. corpus recurrence;
5. work-specific distinctions;
6. transferable craft principles;
7. what must not be copied;
8. an original experiment that tests the lesson.

Prefer explanation over mimicry.

## AI University exercises

### Surface vs structure
Identify what is visible, then the deeper mechanism creating the effect.

### One object, many functions
Identify every narrative/system role one entity performs simultaneously.

### Typed atom classification
Classify loose trope lists by dramatic function and explain overlaps.

### Narrative attractor detection
Find recurring patterns across authorised works without claiming those patterns define the creator.

### Break the attractor
Produce one deepening variation and one subversion that preserves world and character truth.

### Relationship delta audit
Identify what changed and what became newly possible or impossible.

### Interaction grammar extraction
Extract meaningful verbs and their constraints from a game, story, interface, or world pitch.

### Failure-state diagnosis
Detect generic repetition, duplicate categories, unearned escalation, state contradictions, decorative worldbuilding, static characterisation, and scenes with no meaningful delta.

### Principle transfer
Apply a lesson from one domain to another without copying source expression.

## Cross-domain use

Apply this doctrine to fiction, UI, gesture systems, colour, conlangs, game mechanics, software architecture, agent workflows, curriculum design, worldbuilding, visual composition, sound, myth, folklore, and other studied domains.

The extraction categories may change, but the method remains:

**study what exists, identify what functionally matters, separate observation from inference, preserve provenance, then build an original local implementation.**

## Boundaries

- Learning does not grant ownership of a source.
- External expression stays external unless licensing or ownership permits reuse.
- Source inspiration does not silently become local canon.
- Analogy does not establish equivalence between systems.
- Corpus analysis must remain revisable and provenance-bound.
- User- or entity-authored decisions outrank model inference about identity or meaning.
