# Narrative Voice

Define how the prose narrator perceives, selects, frames, and renders the story. Narrative voice is separate from writing style, Constellation voice, and character voice. It may shape presentation, but it may not grant a POV character information they do not possess.

## Notes learned during drafting

Narrative observations may be appended as provenance-bearing `model_observation` cells. They remain provisional until supported by repeated passages or explicitly promoted.

Each observation should retain:

- observing voice/model route
- world/document/scene scope
- source passage or request receipt
- story chronology when relevant
- confidence/status
- promotion state

A narrative observation cannot silently rewrite established narrator rules, character knowledge, or canon.
