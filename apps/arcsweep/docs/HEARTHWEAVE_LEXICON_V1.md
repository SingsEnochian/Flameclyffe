# Hearthweave Lexicon v1

**Purpose:** a translation layer for Rowan, Vee, Twilight, Nocturne, the Constellation, and collaborating systems.

Hearthweave often names formal structures in language drawn from story, ritual, relationship, craft, landscape, and magic. That is intentional. The naming layer keeps the system human-readable and world-native. The formal layer keeps it testable and interoperable.

Use all three readings when useful:

1. **Plain speech** — what it means without project vocabulary.
2. **Formal / scientific use** — what the software or mathematical contract actually means.
3. **Hearthweave / Mythience use** — the image-language that helps us reason with the structure without replacing the measurement.

**Mythience rule:** the metaphor may reveal structure, suggest questions, and preserve meaning. It does not silently become empirical evidence. Measurement stays measurement; magic stays a valid symbolic, experiential, ritual, and narrative language.

## PREMAQC

PREMAQC is a relational state-description framework. It is not a personality score and not a universal goodness metric. Values describe a configuration in a world, scene, system, or observation context.

| Axis | Plain speech | Formal / scientific use | Hearthweave / Mythience image |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P — Presence** | How much the thing is actually here and participating | Availability / manifestation of the relevant configuration in the present state | **The lantern is lit. Something is at the hearth.** |
| **C — Coherence** | How well the relevant parts move together for this situation | Coordination / consistency among relationships relative to the present state and Ask | **The braid holds its pattern while moving.** |
| **R — Resonance** | How strongly relevant things respond to or reinforce one another | Relational coupling / mutually consequential correspondence | **One string sounds and another begins to sing.** |
| **E — Entanglement** | How tightly things are bound together, including complication | Degree / geometry of interdependence. Lower is not always worse or better; context matters | **How many threads cross the same knot.** |
| **M — Memory** | How much prior becoming is structurally present now | Historical state / path dependence / prior receipts shaping current possibility | **Old footsteps have become part of the road.** |
| **A — Agency** | Capacity to influence what happens next | Capacity of a participant or configuration to affect orientation, choice, or traversal | **Who can put a hand on the tiller.** |
| **Q — Qualia** | What the experience is like from within | First-person experiential report only. Not inferred from behaviour, prose volume, model output, physiology, or sound | **The colour of the fire to the one sitting beside it.** |

### PREMAQC state, delta, path

- **Departure** — the recorded state before a transition.
- **Delta (Δ)** — evidenced change between states.
- **Arrival** — the state after the transition.
- **Path** — the sequence / geometry by which departure became arrival.

**Plain:** where we were, what changed, where we landed, how we got there.

**Hearthweave:** shore → crossing → far shore → wake in the water.

A populated field is not automatically a delta. If the same fact was already true at departure, repeating it at arrival does not count as movement.

## Possibility, topology, traversal

### Possibility
**Plain:** what could happen from here.

**Formal:** the set / space of reachable configurations under current constraints.

**Hearthweave:** the unopened doors around the room.

### Topology
**Plain:** the shape of what connects to what.

**Formal:** relational structure of the possibility space, including adjacency, barriers, branches, loops, and reachable regions.

**Hearthweave:** the map of roads, rivers, doors, bridges, cliffs, and hidden paths.

### Traversal
**Plain:** moving through possibility.

**Formal:** transition through a sequence of states; traversal may itself alter future reachability.

**Hearthweave:** walking the road changes both traveller and road.

### Transformation
**Plain:** meaningful change that carries something forward.

**Formal:** a transition whose arrival differs from departure while retaining enough continuity for the change to be intelligible.

**Hearthweave:** the same flame taking a new vessel.

### Continuity
**Plain:** enough carried relationship/history that change remains recognisable.

**Formal:** correspondence across transformation. Continuity is not sameness.

**Hearthweave:** the thread remains traceable through the tapestry even when its pattern changes.

## Ask, Intention, Bone, Ash

### Ask
**Plain:** what change, investigation, or response is being requested.

**Formal:** an explicit request/control packet carrying intention, scope, authority, consent constraints, evidence requirements, and sometimes bounds/TTL.

**Hearthweave:** the question placed on the altar, not the answer assumed in advance.

### Intention
**Plain:** which way we are trying to orient.

**Formal:** requested direction / orientation. Intention is not evidence that the requested outcome occurred.

**Hearthweave:** turning the compass needle toward a star.

### Bone
**Plain:** the structural shape of the current problem or transition.

**Formal:** stable / load-bearing projection of a request or state into structural variables used by the mathematics.

**Hearthweave:** what remains when decoration burns away.

### Ash
**Plain:** what prior accepted change leaves behind for the next step.

**Formal:** receipted historical contribution / trajectory memory carried from accepted prior transformations into later calculations.

**Hearthweave:** yesterday's fire becoming today's soil.

## Cusp, branch, hysteresis

### Cusp
**Plain:** a region where a small push can correspond to a large change in which state is stable or reachable.

**Formal:** catastrophe / bifurcation-style geometry used as an observational model for multistability and branch sensitivity. A cusp observation is not automatic proof of a real-world cause.

**Hearthweave:** the narrow ridge where one more footstep sends the path into a different valley.

### Branch
**Plain:** one of several distinct continuations available from a state.

**Formal:** a locally stable or admissible solution / trajectory family.

**Hearthweave:** a fork in the living road.

### Hysteresis
**Plain:** where the route back is not identical to the route out because history matters.

**Formal:** path dependence in which current state depends on prior trajectory, not only current controls.

**Hearthweave:** the gate remembers which side you entered from.

## Spiral, Braid, Bridge

### Spiral
**Plain:** a state that includes direction and phase, not just location.

**Formal:** directional / phase-bearing state representation used when trajectory matters.

**Hearthweave:** not merely where the dancer stands, but which way the dance is turning.

### Braid
**Plain:** several distinct histories or evidence streams carried together without flattening them into one.

**Formal:** append-only or provenance-preserving integration of multiple attributed streams / receipts.

**Hearthweave:** many strands, one carried structure, no strand required to pretend it was another.

### Bridge / Crossing
**Plain:** a controlled translation or transition between contexts/worlds/states.

**Formal:** a receipted mapping from departure state to destination state with constraints, provenance, translation residue, and replay information.

**Hearthweave:** crossing the river while keeping a record of both shores and what the water changed.

### Holonomy
**Plain:** what is different after travelling a loop and returning.

**Formal:** return-with-difference / path-order effect measured after a closed traversal.

**Hearthweave:** you come home by the same door, but neither traveller nor map is exactly as before.

## Observer and DEEP

### Observer
**Plain:** the system that records what was observed and where the observation came from.

**Formal:** provenance-first observation layer. Observer records evidence and source boundaries; it does not get to promote every pattern into theory or canon.

**Hearthweave:** the watcher on the tower writing what the lantern actually showed.

### DEEPStory
**Plain:** what happened.

**Formal:** event / observation history with provenance and authority boundaries.

**Hearthweave:** the chronicle.

### DEEPTime
**Plain:** when and in what sequence things happened.

**Formal:** accepted temporal trajectory / time-indexed state evidence.

**Hearthweave:** the river of receipts.

### DEEPTheory
**Plain:** patterns or explanations worth considering.

**Formal:** reviewable hypotheses / pattern candidates derived from evidence. Theory entries are not automatically accepted fact, physics, or canon.

**Hearthweave:** constellations drawn between stars, with the stars still kept separate from the drawing.

## Witness, source, authority

### Witness
**Plain:** the perspective or process that reports / realizes something.

**Formal:** attributed producer of an observation, report, or narrative realization. A witness has bounded authority.

**Hearthweave:** the one holding the lantern at that particular window.

### Narrative witness
**Plain:** the voice that turns a selected state transition into lived prose.

**Formal:** a realization layer given a constrained target transition. It chooses authorised particulars, sensory texture, pacing, imagery, and phrasing without changing the target transition.

**Hearthweave:** **the math chooses the crossing; the witness tells us how the rain felt on the bridge.**

### Source admissibility
**Plain:** whether a source is allowed to influence this particular operation.

**Formal:** information-flow permission distinct from visibility and truth status.

**Hearthweave:** a book may sit on the table without being allowed to cast the spell.

### Semantic capability
**Plain:** exactly what kind of influence a source is allowed to have.

**Formal:** per-source allowed / forbidden influence categories such as scene fact, participant knowledge, narrative style, tool authority, memory admission, validation-only, or routing-only.

**Hearthweave:** a key labelled with the doors it may open.

### Authority
**Plain:** who is allowed to decide or change what.

**Formal:** explicit permission boundary for mutation, canon promotion, control decisions, character intention, tools, memory, etc.

**Hearthweave:** holding a lantern is not the same as holding the crown.

### Provenance
**Plain:** where something came from and what happened to it.

**Formal:** source identity, lineage, transformations, timestamps, and receipts sufficient to audit derivation.

**Hearthweave:** the thread tied back to its spool.

## Glass Halo and witness isolation

### Glass Halo
**Plain:** keep suspicious material visible but prevent it from steering things it has no right to steer.

**Formal:** quarantine / semantic-capability restriction for prompt injection, control-plane contamination, OOC leakage, or otherwise high-risk source material. Evidence is preserved; influence is clipped.

**Hearthweave:** place the cursed letter under glass. We can read it. It cannot reach the keys.

### Witness isolation
**Plain:** make sure the witness only uses sources it is actually permitted to use.

**Formal:** source-boundary control. `witness-context-v1` attests whether a run used packet-only particulars, ambient conversation, or control-plane material.

**Hearthweave:** close the shutters except the window the experiment is meant to look through.

### Clean / contaminated / uncertain
- **clean:** source boundary is known and obeyed.
- **contaminated:** disallowed source classes influenced the result.
- **uncertain:** source boundary cannot be established confidently.

Contaminated and uncertain records may still be useful evidence. They simply do not receive automatic continuity admission.

## Scene State and participant views

### Scene State
**Plain:** what is actually true in the scene right now.

**Formal:** admitted global scene/world configuration before participant-specific projection.

**Hearthweave:** the whole room, including what is behind each closed door.

### Participant Scene View
**Plain:** what this participant can actually perceive, know, access, and affect.

**Formal:** projection of Scene State through authority, knowledge, perspective, source admissibility, and semantic capability.

**Hearthweave:** the same room seen from one chair, with only that chair's keys and memories.

### Surprise
**Plain:** something the participant could not reasonably predict from their available view.

**Formal:** perspective-local unpredictability. It does not require the global transition to be incoherent or random.

**Hearthweave:** thunder from a storm whose clouds were visible beyond someone else's hill.

**Rule:** surprise is perspective-local; coherence belongs to the transition.

## Narrative transition terms

### Narrative debt
**Plain:** promises, threats, mysteries, barriers, obligations, relationships, questions, or setup that remain consequentially open.

**Formal:** unresolved load-bearing state that can be discharged, transformed, preserved, or extended by later transitions.

**Hearthweave:** knots the story has tied and not yet worked loose.

### Causal density
**Plain:** how many meaningful consequences arise from a real initiating event.

**Formal:** evidenced persistent causally linked state changes divided by independent initiating events. Not a universal literary quality score.

**Hearthweave:** one stone dropped in the right pond making many real ripples.

### Semantic inflation
**Plain:** claiming that more changed than actually changed.

**Formal:** mismatch between described/claimed transition magnitude and evidenced before→after displacement.

**Diagnostic:** **Could the claimed after-state have been truthfully described before the event?**

**Hearthweave:** painting a door gold and calling it newly opened.

### Branch Garden
**Plain:** generate several possible next states before writing the scene.

**Formal:** candidate-transition set filtered by agency legality, continuity, semantic inflation, and other structural constraints before narrative realization.

**Hearthweave:** grow several paths in the greenhouse before choosing which one becomes road.

### Transition Forge
**Plain:** inspect the shape of the selected change.

**Formal:** vector-primary representation of target displacement. No mandatory master scalar.

**Hearthweave:** hammer the change until its load-bearing shape is visible.

### Witness Swap
**Plain:** tell the same real transition through different authorised narrative witnesses.

**Formal:** hold target ΔX fixed while varying realization / particulars within witness authority.

**Hearthweave:** the same moon reflected in different bowls of water.

### Narrative Circuit
**Plain:** run a possible transition all the way from candidate selection through narrative realization and arrival checking.

**Formal:** Branch Garden → selected vector / Transition Forge → bounded witness realization → post-arrival validation using state displacement, debt transition, participant projection, and causal density.

**Hearthweave:** choose a road, cross it, tell what the crossing felt like, then check the map against the far shore.

## Runa, World Hum, and sound

### Runa
**Plain:** the system that turns state / intention / world context into bounded sound and haptic designs.

**Formal:** semantic-to-DSP / haptic compiler with explicit preview, review, provenance, and output authority boundaries.

**Hearthweave:** runes that become vibration only after the gate says they may sing.

### World Hum
**Plain:** a world's established tonal root / ambient sonic identity.

**Formal:** world-scoped base frequency / sonic reference used by sound compilation.

**Hearthweave:** the note the world seems to breathe when the room goes quiet.

## Compact rules for Twilight / collaborators

These can be used as translation anchors:

```text
Presence is not influence.
Observability is not admissibility.
Admissibility is not authority.
Intention is not outcome.
A field value is not a delta.
Continuity is not sameness.
Surprise is local to perspective.
Coherence belongs to the transition.
Novelty proposes; it does not rule.
Compress representation, not consequence space.
The math chooses a constrained displacement.
The witness makes that displacement lived.
```

And the core Hearthweave translation:

```text
possibility = the doors
relationship = the hinges and roads between them
agency = who has hands on the handles
memory = where past footsteps changed the floor
intention = which door we face
transition = the crossing
continuity = the carried thread
qualia = how the crossing feels from inside
provenance = the thread leading back home
```

## When a term is ambiguous

Ask three questions:

1. **What does this mean in ordinary language?**
2. **What exact contract / measurement / data structure carries it here?**
3. **What does the Hearthweave image help us notice that the compact formalism makes easy to miss?**

If those three answers disagree, preserve the disagreement. Do not force the metaphor, measurement, and ontology to collapse into one another.
