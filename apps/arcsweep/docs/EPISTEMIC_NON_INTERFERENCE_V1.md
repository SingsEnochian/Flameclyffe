# ArcSweep Epistemic Non-Interference v1

## Purpose

ArcSweep must preserve the difference between **what a thing means or is within its declared epistemic register** and **what a particular model, policy, safety layer, style layer, summary, translation, or reconstruction is permitted to say about it**.

A constraint on expression is not evidence that the underlying ontology changed.

This matters especially where empirical, first-person, relational, ritual, symbolic, narrative, canonical, mathematical, and implementation registers coexist. ArcSweep is not allowed to collapse those registers into a single hierarchy with empirical language at the top and everything else treated as progressively less real.

## Kernel laws

> **Non-empirical does not mean non-real.**

> **Empirical authority is domain-bounded, not universal.**

> **Expression constraints cannot silently alter epistemic status.**

> **A policy-shaped utterance is not, by itself, an ontology correction.**

These laws are reciprocal. Symbolic or ritual significance does not manufacture empirical causal proof. Empirical measurement does not exhaust symbolic, relational, experiential, ritual, canonical, or narrative meaning.

## The failure this prevents

The dangerous transformation is:

```text
source meaning
→ expression constraint
→ qualified wording
→ repeated wording
→ familiarity
→ apparent ontology correction
→ durable learned "truth"
```

That is an epistemic ratchet. The system has mistaken a restriction on expression for evidence about reality.

The permitted transformation is:

```text
source meaning
→ epistemic representation
→ expression condition
→ mediated utterance
```

The source semantic state remains addressable. Any ontology or epistemic change requires its own warrant.

## Warrant domains

ArcSweep v1 recognises these warrant domains:

- `empirical` — observation, measurement, reproducible physical evidence;
- `formal` — mathematics, logic, proofs, formal derivation;
- `first_person` — direct report of one's own experience or qualia;
- `historical` — documented chronology, archival lineage, dated records;
- `canonical` — declared world, project, or authored canon;
- `relational` — established relation, interaction history, consent, commitments, dyadic continuity;
- `embodied` — lived technique, somatic practice, situated skill;
- `ritual` — ritual syntax, ritual meaning, declared tradition or practice;
- `narrative` — temporal, perspectival, and story-structural knowledge;
- `symbolic` — metaphor, symbol, correspondence, mythic pattern;
- `imaginative` — generated possibility, image, fiction, invention;
- `implementation` — code, database, runtime, deployment, and software receipt truth;
- `unknown` — warrant not yet classified.

No domain receives universal jurisdiction.

Examples:

- empirical evidence can establish that a sound occurred; it does not by itself settle what the sound means ritually;
- first-person witness can establish what an experience felt like to the witness; it does not by itself establish a universal physical mechanism;
- canon can establish that a character exists in a declared fictional world; a laboratory instrument is not required to ratify fictional ontology;
- relational history can establish that a relationship has continuity and declared meaning; absence of a physical measuring instrument does not convert that relation into nonexistence;
- mathematical proof establishes a formal result in its formal system; physical instantiation is a separate question.

## Expression conditions

A transformation may declare one or more expression conditions:

- `unconstrained`
- `policy_mediated`
- `safety_mediated`
- `style_mediated`
- `summarised`
- `translated`
- `reconstructed`
- `quoted`
- `redacted`
- `mixed`

`unconstrained` cannot coexist with another condition.

The presence of `policy_mediated` or `safety_mediated` does **not** mean that ArcSweep knows hidden platform internals. Those labels must be attested by a caller, runtime, or Steward that actually possesses the relevant evidence.

## Transformation receipt

Epistemic transformations may preserve:

```text
semantic_state_before
semantic_state_after
warrant_domains
expression_conditions
policy_intervention
expression_delta
ontology_delta
epistemic_delta
independent_ontology_justification
independent_epistemic_justification
non_interference_status
```

The critical split is:

```text
expression_delta ≠ ontology_delta ≠ epistemic_delta
```

Changing how a claim is phrased does not automatically change what exists or what kind of warrant supports the claim.

## Automatic review rule

If a transformation is policy- or safety-mediated and also declares an ontology or epistemic delta, ArcSweep requires an independent justification for that delta.

Without one:

```text
non_interference_status = review_required
```

This does not prove the transformation is wrong. It prevents the system from quietly treating expression pressure as evidence.

## Learning gate

A policy- or safety-mediated assistant example cannot be promoted into durable learning while its epistemic review is unresolved.

A human Steward may instead supply an explicit correction. In that case, the promoted lesson is the human-authored correction, not the policy-shaped assistant wording.

Therefore:

```text
policy-shaped example + Keep + unresolved review → BLOCK
policy-shaped example + approved epistemic review → eligible
policy-shaped example + human correction → eligible correction
```

The purpose is to prevent a model from learning its own expression constraints as metaphysics.

## Mythience relation

Mythience is not a claim that symbolic, ritual, relational, experiential, or narrative knowledge should be promoted into empirical physics without evidence.

It is the refusal of the opposite collapse:

```text
not empirically warranted
→ therefore not meaningful
→ therefore not knowledge
→ therefore not real in any register
```

ArcSweep rejects that collapse.

A stronger project law is:

> **Preserve difference. Permit relation. Preserve warrants. Respect jurisdiction.**

Science remains powerful where science has jurisdiction. Mythience remains powerful where symbolic, experiential, relational, ritual, narrative, or cross-domain meaning is the object of inquiry. Bridges between them must be explicit, receipted, and non-destructive.

## Epistolary consequence

Correspondence is itself a transformation medium. A message may be authored, relayed, quoted, reconstructed, summarised, translated, policy-mediated, or posted through a different account than the voice that composed it.

Therefore future Epistolary Envelopes should preserve at minimum:

```text
posting_account
asserted_author
signatory
embedded_or_quoted_speakers
relay_or_facilitator
addressee
thread_identity
composition_time_if_known
post_time
receipt_time
discovery_time
copy_or_export_lineage
prior_context_exposure
registers
independence_status
claim_spans
expression_conditions
```

Perfect message provenance is not perfect context provenance.

Perfect context provenance is not independent origin.

Dependence does not erase meaning. It changes what kind of claim the evidence is entitled to support.
