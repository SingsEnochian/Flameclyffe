# Frontier Risk and Governance proving corpus

This fixture directory is intentionally source-light. Do not commit copied article bodies or social-media threads merely to make a demo self-contained.

For each proving run, provide one primary warning and at least two independent responses/analyses as source records carrying stable URI, publication time, and SHA-256 content hash. Preserve quotation limits and source licences.

The ingest must classify individual claims rather than assigning one confidence value to an entire source. Forecasts, rhetoric, and policy prescriptions remain distinct from observations and testimony. Actor incentive profiles describe structural pressures and information position; they are not motive claims.

A passing run must emit a `arcsweep.swarm-epistemic-synthesis/v0.1` receipt and retain specialist disagreement, unresolved assumptions, and source independence. No proving result is canonical World or canonical theory state.
