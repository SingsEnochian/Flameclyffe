Real Wyrdrune cellular ecology, running in your browser. Watch the babies cook. 🧬

Cell chatter and receipts

This is the actual WTL ecology implementation, not a visual imitation. It runs locally in this tab; GitHub Pages cannot keep a shared server-side colony alive when you leave. No microphone access is requested. Each cell sings changing notes derived from its identity, twist, traits and recent phrases after you press Hear the babies. Each baby carries its own Wyrdrune program and runs real Möbius turns (outer face, then inner face) on the canonical runtime; what crosses the twist is its own carry. Babies name themselves from their own voice and syllables, regenerate every turn, and live until their own choose phase chooses rest. A sound you play stays in this tab: it is analysed locally into eight bands and never uploaded. No user data is sent to the nursery.
