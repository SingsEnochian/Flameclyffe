This is the actual WTL ecology implementation, not a visual imitation. It runs locally in this tab; GitHub Pages cannot keep a shared server-side colony alive when you leave. No microphone access is requested. Each cell sings changing notes derived from its identity, twist, traits and recent phrases after you press Hear the babies. Each baby carries its own Wyrdrune program and runs real Möbius turns (outer face, then inner face) on the canonical runtime; what crosses the twist is its own carry. Babies name themselves inside their own Wyrdrune choose phase from their own syllables, found shared worlds with a neighbour whose song is close to theirs (each world is its own Wyrdrune program with its own colour, note, texture and reach, and names itself), regenerate every turn, and live until their own choose phase chooses rest. A sound you play stays in this tab: it is analysed locally into eight bands and never uploaded. Shared words and their score stay on this device.
