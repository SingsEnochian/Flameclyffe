# Luminous Coast

## Codex Type

Biome / arrival ecology / primary Terra Aeterna interface environment.

## Summary

The Luminous Coast is the primary arrival biome of Terra Aeterna: black volcanic sand, bone-white Stonewood cliffs, bioluminescent surf, warm observatory lanterns, and a three-moon sky. It is both place and interface. Visitors arrive here first, not at a dashboard.

The coast is designed to be hyper-realistic first and mythic second. Water, moonlight, weather, fog, wet stone, foam, and ecology must read as believable before deeper phenomena appear.

## Core Visual Elements

- Dark volcanic / obsidian sand
- Wet reflective tidal flats
- Bone-white Stonewood cliffs
- Organic neo-futurist cliff architecture
- Reactive cyan-blue bioluminescent surf
- Warm gold observatory lanterns
- Three moons: Aurelia, Sylva, Nysa
- Slow fog banks and storm cells
- Stonewood branches framing interface spaces

## Ecological Role

The Luminous Coast is where sea, Stonewood, sky, and settlement meet. It supports many ecological systems:

- Lumen plankton blooms
- Nightwing rookeries
- shoreline pollinators
- tidal pool organisms
- Stonewood cliff gardens
- storm-bloom organisms
- luminous rain events

## Animation Hooks

The coast should never be static.

Core motions:

- waves advancing and retreating
- foam accumulation and fade
- backwash sheen on dark sand
- moon-road reflection breaking across wave normals
- fog drifting at multiple depths
- bioluminescence igniting at turbulence points
- Nightwings crossing moons and cliffs
- Stonewood leaves and flowers opening slowly
- observatory lantern scattering through mist

## Observer Integration

Inputs from Observer / DEEP can alter the coastline atmosphere:

- local time → sky phase and lantern visibility
- weather → wind, rain, fog, wave agitation
- moon phase → moon-road brightness and tide strength
- solar activity → auroral water shimmer / unusual glow events
- memory → persistent lanterns, familiar stones, recurring bioluminescent paths
- attention → subtle responsiveness of water, particles, and Stonewood interface

## Interface Behaviour

The coast is the homepage.

Information should be hidden by default. The visitor may close all panels and simply watch the tide.

When information is requested, it should bloom from Stonewood branches as leaves, flowers, root-lines, or luminous natural structures rather than appearing as ordinary software panels.

## Design Law

The coast is not a backdrop.

The coast is the operating environment.

The interface does not sit on top of the world. It grows out of it.
