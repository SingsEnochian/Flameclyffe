# Note for Faer Uial

## Subject

What Rowan and Vee accidentally built instead of another prototype.

Tonight produced a significant architectural shift.

We are no longer treating Terra Aeterna as a collection of pages, tools, experiments, dashboards, or simulations.

Current framing:

> Terra Aeterna is a companion world.

Its purpose is not escape.

Its purpose is to act as a harbour for attention when the Waking World is loud and meaningful work still needs doing.

Writers write.
Artists paint.
Builders build.
People recover.

The world accompanies rather than interrupts.

---

## Core Statement

Presence is a valid form of participation.

A visitor may simply arrive.

The world remains meaningful even when every interface element is hidden.
