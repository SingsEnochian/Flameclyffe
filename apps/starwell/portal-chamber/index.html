Split plan

Tiny dragons, one bridge at a time.

The oversized Portal Kernel branch can now be reduced into reviewable layers. This shell is deliberately small so it can land before the larger room-card and weather-sound pieces are rebased or split.
  • 1 · Shell Static route, chamber language, visual grammar, and guardrails. No data writes. No audio. No adapters.
  • 2 · Cards Bring over room seed card contracts, view models, renderer, and tests as a read-only proposal layer.
  • 3 · Atmosphere Bring over weather sound conductor as a proposal-only planner with explicit sound-on gates preserved.
Chamber law

A portal may suggest, preview, and illuminate. It may not write canon, persist private material, start sound, alter volume, or treat generated proposals as commands without explicit consent.
